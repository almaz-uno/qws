== Approach

*Held at the unmap.* `handle` of an `UnmapNotify` of the window or of its
frame asks `hidden`, with whether the window was viewable before the event
and which window was unmapped. A window that was viewable, changed
(`schedule.dirty`), not stale, with a pixmap named — its own, or its frame's
when the frame is the window unmapped — keeps the pixmap, and its binding on
the GPU, marked `last`; any other is released and made stale, as now.

*Due at once.* `window.due` is the time a window is to be captured: a
`last` window at once, any other as its schedule says. `nextDue` and
`captureChanged` ask it instead of the schedule, so a `last` window is
captured at the next turn of the loop, or, while paused, when the loop
captures again: at the end of the pause, or at an activation, whose
`captureChanged` takes every window due. Nothing else of the loop changes.

*The snapshot from what is held.* `capture` of a window not viewable takes,
when it is `last`, the snapshot from the pixmap held — `captureHeld`: the
pixmap bound on the GPU, after `WaitX`, bound again and averaged, as
`captureGPU` does with a pixmap bound; else by RENDER, at `pixmapRect`, as
`capture` does for a pixmap the GPU did not bind — stores it, records the
shot in the schedule, logs "Snapshot" with cause `hidden`; then, `last` or
not, releases the pixmap and makes the window stale, as now. A window shown
again before it is captured is viewable again and captured as any, which
clears `last`.

*The frame kept at its size.* `namePixmap` keeps the size of the ancestor
whose pixmap it names (`viaSize`) beside its border, which it keeps
already. `handle` of a `ConfigureNotify` of that ancestor makes the window
stale, and changed, only when the width, the height or the border width
differs from them.

== Kept

* The schedule (`schedule.go`) and its tests: `due` of the window wraps it.
* The live passes: `livePassable` passes viewable windows only; a `last`
  window is not one.
* `forget` and `release` as they are: a `last` window destroyed, or gone
  from the client list, frees its pixmap with the rest.
