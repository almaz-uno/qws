== What and why

The CPU draws a frame of the grid of E1 in 350–385 ms, against 55–75 ms for
the carousel (specification 007, research, "The grid"). With `cpu` every
step of the grid waits for such a frame; with `glx` the step moves on time,
but its frame at rest comes 208–449 ms after the frame before it, and the
tiles layer of the animation, `GridTiles`, costs as much again.
Specification 001 made the carousel fast by means that keep every byte; the
grid kept drawing its tiles one after another, every thumbnail resampled in
place and every long title measured once for each prefix tried (research,
"Where the time goes").

The change draws the same bytes in about a ninth of the time. The part of
each tile under its title is drawn in parallel, apart from the canvas, and
copied in when the canvas allows it; a title is truncated measuring each of
its runes once; a face keeps the advances of its glyphs.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `575d2ee`. Unchanged: every
pixel of every frame and layer `cpu` draws — the grid, the carousel, the
layers of the animation — and so of `glx`; the layout of the grid; the
configuration, its keys, environment variables, flags and defaults. Nothing
a user can see changes but the time the grid takes to draw.

== Decisions

Made on the way, 2026-10-01, for the author to confirm; each keeps every
byte, as `TestScenes` checks:

* *The heads of the tiles.* The part of a tile under its title — the shadow
  of a selected or a hovered tile, the background, the thumbnail and its
  border, the icon, the bar under an urgent title — is its head; the title,
  the workspace and the frame are its tail. The heads are drawn in parallel
  into a scratch canvas of the size of the frame, each in a rectangle that
  holds every pixel it writes, filled first with the pixel of the canvas
  under it. A tile then copies its head into the canvas if the canvas is
  still that pixel in the whole rectangle; otherwise it draws the head in
  place, as before. Tiles whose rectangles meet draw theirs in place. The
  tail is drawn in place, in order. Why the bytes are the same: research,
  "What keeps the bytes", and plan, "Why it is exact".
* *Half of the CPUs* draw the heads, `GOMAXPROCS/2`, at least one: the
  frame of E1 takes as long as with a worker on every CPU, for less CPU, and
  the other half stays with the drawing and the loop of the animation
  (research, "Workers").
* *The thumbnails are resampled before the canvas is made*, with
  `prepareOpaque` of specification 001; the heads start when the canvas and
  the header are drawn.
* *The scratch canvas* comes from a `sync.Pool`, not a cache kept for good:
  14 MB a frame more to allocate after a collection, none held by an idle
  instance.
* *`MultiFallbackFace` keeps what `GlyphAdvance` returned* for each rune,
  without a lock: a face is already used by one drawing at a time (`FontSet`,
  specification 007). The carousel and the header measure through it too.

== Behavior

. `DrawGridLayout` and `GridTiles` return the same bytes as on `develop` for
  any windows, selection, hover and configuration.
. `DrawGridLayout` lays the tiles out with `headerBand` before it makes the
  canvas — the value `drawHeader` returns, as `GridTiles` and `GridTile`
  already do — and starts the heads; it then makes the canvas, draws the
  header and draws the tiles in order, each after its head is drawn.
  `GridTiles` does the same on its transparent canvas, without a header.
. A drawing of the grid runs up to `GOMAXPROCS/2` goroutines of its own and
  returns after all of them have ended.
. `truncateTitle` takes the face instead of the context, and measures each
  rune of the title once.

Outside the specification: the carousel's drawing, but for the faces it
shares; the presentation; the exact options of research, "Exact options
not taken"; anything that changes a pixel (research, "What would change
pixels").

== Metrics

M1 — the time of a frame of the grid:: The wall time of one call of
`DrawGridLayout` on the scene `grid-e1-24-second`, and on `grid-e1-header`,
of Σ: `BenchmarkE1Grid` in `pkg/carousel`, on ws1, `-benchtime 20x`, the test
binaries of `develop` and of the change run in turn, three times two runs of
each.

M2 — the time of the tiles layer:: As M1, for `GridTiles` of
`grid-e1-header`: `BenchmarkE1Grid/grid-e1-header-tiles`.

M3 — the time of a frame of the carousel:: As M1, `BenchmarkE1Frame` of
specification 005.

The CPU time of a frame and the bytes it allocates are reported, without a
criterion.

== Acceptance criteria

|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| The picture: `TestScenes` passes with `pkg/carousel/testdata/scenes.json`
  unchanged — the digests of the 24 scenes of Σ, 12 of them of the grid,
  recorded by specification 001. `TestGLXPresenterMatchesCPU` and
  `TestLayers` pass.

| K2
| The heads: `TestGridHeads` — for the grid scenes of Σ and for eight
  configurations beyond them, which put the tiles too close for their heads,
  the shadow into the next tile, a workspace wider than its tile, odd
  thumbnails and icons, tiles smaller than their margins, the corners of a
  large window radius, and a transparent and a square window background —
  `DrawGridLayout` and `GridTiles` give the bytes of the tiles drawn one
  after another with every head in place; in the first six of those
  configurations some head is drawn in place; and no pixel a head changes
  lies outside its rectangle.

| K3
| Drawing in parallel: `TestParallelDrawing`, with frames and tiles layers
  of the grid among its jobs, passes, also under `go test -race`.

| K4
| M1 ≤ 60 ms for each of the two scenes in every run: about a sixth of
  `develop`, comparable to the carousel's 55–75 ms on E1.

| K5
| M2 ≤ 75 ms in every run.

| K6
| M3 of the change no greater than the greatest of `develop` in the same
  session.

| K7
| `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...` and `make check-specs` pass.
