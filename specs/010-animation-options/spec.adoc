== What and why

Specification 007 animated the switcher on `glx` in one way for everyone:
every animation takes 150 ms, the overlay fades in and fades out, the
carousel steps and the selection frame of the grid slides, and the hover
frame appears at once. Changing any of this means switching to `cpu`, which
also gives up the GPU presenter. The author asks (2026-10-01, the open
decisions of 007) for a key that turns the animation off, a duration that
can be set, a zoom for the appearance, a way to turn each effect off on its
own, and the hover frame animated by a fade or by a zoom — which of the two
to be chosen after seeing both on E1.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `0016519` (1.0.0). Unchanged:
the `cpu` renderer, which changes its picture at once whatever the keys say;
the frames at rest of both renderers, byte for byte; the step of 007 and its
duration.

== Decisions

Author, 2026-10-01: the animation can be turned off as a whole; its duration
is a key; the appearance may zoom; the appearance and each other effect can
be turned off on its own; the hover frame can fade or zoom, to be seen.
Having tried the variants on E1 (K10): the zoom of the hover closes in from
1.05 of its size, not 1.1; the defaults are fade and zoom together for the
appearance, the disappearance and the hover.

Made on the way, 2026-10-01, for the author to confirm:

* the keys live under `appearance.animation`; an effect is a list of
  `fade` and `zoom`, so that each can be had alone or both together;
* one duration for every animation;
* the zoom of the appearance runs from 0.92 of the size to the full size,
  and of the disappearance back to 0.92; the hover frame runs from 1.05 of
  its size down to its size as it appears, and back to 1.05 as it goes — it
  closes in on the tile rather than growing out of it; the factors are
  constants, not keys;
* with a motion off, `glx` keeps composing layers: a step shows the scene at
  its target in the next frame and the exact frame once drawn, as at the
  end of an animated step — not a frame drawn on the spot, which takes
  350–480 ms for the grid on E1 (research of 007);
* an unknown effect name or a negative duration is reported with a warning
  and ignored, as are unknown key names elsewhere in the switcher.

== Behavior

All of it on `glx`; `cpu` reads the keys and changes its picture at once.

=== Keys

[cols="2,1,5"]
|===
| Key | Default | Meaning

| `appearance.animation.enabled`
| `true`
| `false`: nothing moves; every change shows at once, as with every effect
  below turned off.

| `appearance.animation.duration`
| `150ms`
| The time of every animation. `0` is as `enabled: false`.

| `appearance.animation.step`
| `true`
| The step: the carousel turns to the next card, the selection frame of the
  grid slides to the next tile. `false`: the selection is at its target in
  the next frame.

| `appearance.animation.show`
| `[fade, zoom]`
| How the overlay appears: a list of effects, `fade` and `zoom`; `[]` or
  `none`, at once.

| `appearance.animation.hide`
| `[fade, zoom]`
| How the overlay disappears; as `show`.

| `appearance.animation.hover`
| `[fade, zoom]`
| How the hover frame appears on the tile or card under the pointer and
  goes from the one the pointer left; as `show`.
|===

An effect list is read from a YAML list, a single name, or names joined by
commas: `show: [fade, zoom]`, `show: zoom`, `QWS_APPEARANCE_ANIMATION_SHOW=fade,zoom`,
`--appearance-animation-show=fade,zoom`. Environment variables and flags are
named as in specification 002.

=== Effects

. *Fade*: every channel of what is shown — the overlay, or the hover frame
  and the shadow of the hovered tile of the grid — is scaled by an alpha
  that runs from 0 to 1 as it appears, from 1 to 0 as it goes, along the
  ease-out cubic of 007 over the duration.
. *Zoom*: what is shown is scaled about its centre, along the same curve
  and in the same frames as the fade: the overlay about the centre of the
  window, from 0.92 to 1 as it appears and from 1 to 0.92 as it goes; the
  hover frame about the centre of its tile or card, from 1.05 to 1 and from
  1 to 1.05, the shadow of the grid with it. Outside the scaled overlay the
  window is transparent.
. With `fade` alone the scale stays 1; with `zoom` alone the alpha stays 1,
  and what goes vanishes when its zoom ends.
. A change mid-way — a new hover before the last has settled, a selection
  during the appearance — starts each motion from where it is, as the
  retargeting of 007.

=== The hover

With `hover` set, a change of the hover starts a motion of the frames: the
frame of the tile or card under the pointer appears, the frame of the one it
left goes, each by the effects of `hover`. Frames follow at the refresh
period, as the animations of 007; the frame at rest, drawn in the
background, follows when the motion ends. In the carousel the hover frame
is a layer of its own, drawn for the card at its offset; during a step it
moves with its card. The hovered tile or card is the one under the pointer,
except the selected one, as at rest. While the layers of the scene are not
yet uploaded, the hover shows at once, as without `hover`.

Without `hover`, the hover shows at once: in the grid through the scene of
layers, as in 007; in the carousel by the frame drawn on the spot, as in
1.0.0.

=== The appearance and the disappearance

`show` and `hide` replace the fade-in and the fade-out of 007, which are
`show: [fade]` and `hide: [fade]`. With `hide` empty, the overlay is
unmapped before the chosen window is activated, as with `cpu`; otherwise
after it, as in 007.

=== Existing configurations

No key, environment variable or flag is renamed or removed. A configuration
without `appearance.animation` gets the defaults: the overlay now zooms as
well as fades as it appears and disappears, and the hover frame fades and
zooms. `show: [fade]`, `hide: [fade]` and `hover: none` give the look of
1.0.0. The changed defaults make the release a minor one (constitution,
"Releases and versions").

== Metrics

As in 007. The frames of a hover motion are logged with the kind `hover`;
the appearance and the disappearance keep the kinds `fade-in` and `fade-out`
whatever their effects.

Session S3:: As S2 of 007, with the grid, and in each activation also a
sweep of the pointer across the middle of the overlay — 40 moves, 60 ms
apart, diagonally from 10 % to 90 % of its width and from 42 % to 58 % of its
height, so as to cross the tiles of the grid, not the gap between two rows —
before the held key; the pointer is then put on the centre of the
focused window, so that focus, which follows the mouse on E1, stays where it
was. `SWEEP=1 HOLD=2 measure.sh run glx <dir>`.

== Open decisions

. The snapshots of one service while another shows its switcher: those of
  1.1.0 cost the frames of a switcher shown by another instance, as each
  pauses only for its own (research, "The control") — to be settled by a
  specification of its own, and the hover measured again then.
. A duration of its own for the hover, should one for all be too slow for
  it.
. The zoom factors as keys, should the constants not suit.
