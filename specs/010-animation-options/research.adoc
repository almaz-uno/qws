== Conditions

E1 is ws1, as in 007: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 SUPER, driver 580.105.08,
one monitor, DP-0, 2560×1440 at 144 Hz — a period of 6.94 ms; i3 with a
compositor; 32 i3 windows, 33 in the switcher; `renderer: glx`, the
author's configuration otherwise. The instance measured is a third one, on
Alt+F11, beside the two services of the author.

Unlike the runs of 007, the services ran 1.1.0 — the build of specification
008, installed at 17:28, which captures every visible window through the GPU
when its contents change. 007 measured beside services of 1.0.0.

The runs are `specs/001-rendering-speed/measure.sh run glx <dir>` with
`HOLD=2`, the layout in `LAYOUT`, `SWEEP=1` for S3, the keys of the
animation in `QWS_APPEARANCE_ANIMATION_*`, and `QWS=/usr/local/bin/qws.v1.0.0`
for the control; 20 activations, 10 for the runs of K4, K5 and K8. The
binary of this change is the build of `c777b19`: the runs before that
commit are of the same code, uncommitted, and the runs after it differ only
by the sweep of `measure.sh`. The logs and summaries are in
`~/.cache/qws-010-runs/20261001-1843/` on ws1, not in the repository.

The intervals above are longer than in 007, where the step of the carousel
had a p95 of 7.09–7.10 ms and 0.45–0.51 % of its intervals missed. Two runs
of S2 back to back tell whether this change or the conditions made them so:

A1 p95 in ms and the share missed. 1.0.0 measures as this change does: the
conditions moved, not the frames. The fade-in is the least steady — its p95
ranges from 7.89 to 15.80 ms over the five runs of this change at 150 ms, as
the uploads of the activation fall on its frames or not.

Without the services the frames are those of 007, to the hundredth of a
millisecond. The services of 1.1.0 capture the visible windows through the
GPU when their contents change — the switcher of the third instance among
them, a frame each refresh — and their captures take the GPU and the X
server from the frames. Each service pauses its own captures while its own
switcher is shown (007), not while another's is: in use, the snapshots of
`qws-workspace.service` run while `qws.service` shows its switcher, and the
other way round. The hover was measured with the services running only.
