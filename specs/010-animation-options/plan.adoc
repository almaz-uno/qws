== Approach

*Keys.* `config.Animation` under `config.Appearance`, with its defaults, flags
and documentation. The selector turns it into the durations of its motions
and the effects of the appearance, the disappearance and the hover, once,
when it is created; names it does not know are reported there, as the
keybindings are.

*Durations.* A motion carries its duration; at 0 it is at its target at
once. A motion turned off keeps its path through the loop: a step at
duration 0 is a step whose first frame is at rest — the scene at the target,
then the frame at rest — so the grid does not fall back to the frame drawn
on the spot.

*The fade of the overlay* becomes `carousel.Fade{Alpha, Scale}`, passed
wherever an alpha was. The GLX presenter scales about the centre of the
window: the present pass, at a scale other than 1, samples the frame
through a linear sampler at the point the scale maps the fragment to, and
is transparent outside the frame; at 1 it fetches texels as now, so the
frame at rest is untouched. The scene scales the corners of its quads in
the vertex shader. The motion of the fade becomes a level from 0 to 1;
`show` and `hide` map it to the alpha and the scale.

*The hover* becomes a level per tile or card, in a map: 1 for the hovered,
0 for the rest, moved by a motion of the hover's duration — 0 without
`hover`. The grid draws the hover shadow and frame of every tile with a
level above 0, at the alpha and the scale the effects give. The carousel
gets a hover layer per card and offset — the frame `drawHoverIndicator`
draws, on a transparent image — drawn in the background like the card
layers and placed after its card, in the z-order of the carousel, only
while `hover` is set. A hover motion runs in the loop like a step: frames at
the refresh period, then the frame at rest.
