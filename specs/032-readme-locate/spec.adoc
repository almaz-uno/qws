== What and why

The animated picture at the top of the README, `doc/images/qws.webp`, is
made by `console/readme-demo` with `make readme-images` (specification
029): a step of the carousel, `q` to the grid, a step of the grid, `q`
back, its frames in motion composed of the layers of `pkg/carousel` as the
presenter of `glx` composes them. It was made before specification 028:
after `q` it shows the grid with its selection frame at rest at once.
Since 1.5.0 qws shows more after a switch to the grid: the selection frame
and the shadow of the selected tile start scaled by `locate_zoom` about the
tile's centre and transparent, and within `locate_duration` shrink onto
the tile and become opaque, eased out (028, D1). The picture shows
behaviour qws no longer has — an open decision of 029.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `83a5551` (the notes of
1.5.2). Unchanged: the code of qws — `cmd/`, `internal/`, `pkg/`; the
stills `doc/images/carousel.png` and `grid.png`; every frame the picture
has now, with its duration and in its order; `README.md` but for the
description of the picture.

== Decisions

D1. *The convergence drawn as the presenter composes it.* After `q` the
picture shows the frames of the convergence: each the scene of `gridItems`
of `pkg/ui` — the shadow of the selected tile, the tiles, the selection
frame — with the shadow and the frame at the look `locateLook` of `pkg/ui`
gives them at that time: their level v = easeOut(t / d) of a motion from 0
to 1 in the duration d (`easeOut`, `motion`); faded by v and zoomed by
z + (1 − z)·v from the scale z (`effects.look` with both effects) about the
centre of the tile (`zoomRect`); the tiles as they are. Each scene composed
on the CPU as `present_glx_scene.go` composes it on the GPU, by the
`composeScene` of 029. The program uses the exported API of `pkg/carousel`
only, and `internal/config` for the defaults, as it does: the few lines of
`pkg/ui` it follows are copied, as it copies `easeOut`, `carouselItems`
and `placeLayer`, and a test holds the copy to their formula (K1).
Rejected: exporting them from `pkg/ui`, the code of qws changed for a
picture; frames read back from the presenter of `glx` itself, which needs
an X server and a GPU that `make readme-images` does not.

D2. *The defaults.* `locate_duration` 400 ms and `locate_zoom` 1.6, from
`config.Default()`, as the program takes the duration of a step from it.
No flag of the program for them.

D3. *Frames at 20 ms,* as those of the steps: the scenes at t = 0, 20, …,
380 ms from the key, 20 frames each shown 20 ms, the first at the key — in
qws the switch presents the first frame of the convergence at once, the
shadow and the frame transparent at the scale 1.6 —; from 400 ms the frame
at rest of the grid, as qws presents its frame at rest once the level is
1. The frames at rest held as long as now: the grid at rest 900 ms after
the convergence, before the step down; the loop 0.4 s longer. Rejected:
the hold of the grid shortened by 400 ms to keep the loop's length, which
leaves the grid at rest half a second before the step.

D4. *The picture as in 029.* Lossless WebP of `img2webp`, at most 3 MB —
the program refuses a larger one; the frames at rest those of `cpu`; the
same bytes on every run. The description of the picture in `README.md`, its
`alt`, names the convergence.

== Acceptance criteria

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| The scenes: `TestLocateFrames` in `console/readme-demo` — after the
  switch to the grid, 20 scenes, at 0, 20, …, 380 ms; in the scene at t the
  shadow of the selected tile and the selection frame at the alpha
  v = 1 − (1 − t/d)³ and zoomed by z + (1 − z)·v about the tile's centre, at
  d = 400 ms and z = 1.6 of `config.Default()`: the formula of `easeOut`,
  `effects.look` and `zoomRect` of `pkg/ui`, to which `TestLocateScene` of
  `pkg/ui` holds qws (028, K1). The alpha growing and the scale shrinking
  at every frame; at t = 0 the alpha 0 and the scale 1.6, at d opaque at 1;
  the tiles as at rest; nothing else in the scene.

| K2
| The frames at rest: every frame of the picture of `83a5551` in the new
  one, pixel for pixel, with its duration and in its order; the frames
  added, those of the convergence, of 20 ms each, between the carousel at
  rest before `q` and the grid at rest after it — the frame after the last
  of the convergence the grid at rest as before. Method: both pictures
  decoded frame by frame with Pillow (`python3-pil`, which seeks animated
  WebP), the RGBA of each frame compared.

| K3
| The picture: `qws.webp` at most 3 MB; 55 frames, 6.76 s — the 35 frames
  and 6.36 s of 029 (Results, K2), 20 frames and 0.4 s more.

| K4
| The stills: `carousel.png` and `grid.png` byte for byte as committed.

| K5
| The same bytes: `make readme-images` twice, in a worktree of its own,
  gives the committed `doc/images/` byte for byte.

| K6
| `go build ./...`, `gofmt -l` empty, `go vet ./...`, `make check-specs` and
  `go test ./...` on every commit, in a worktree of its own.
|===

== Open decisions

. The header of the pictures shows `v1.4.1`, the default of the program's
  `-version`; another version changes the stills, a change of its own.
. The open decisions 2 and 3 of 029 stay open: the constitution lists the
  programs of `console/` and the targets of the build without
  `readme-demo` and `make readme-images`; `doc/configuration.asciidoc`
  gives `current` as the default of `windows.workspace`, the code `all`.
