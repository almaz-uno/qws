== What and why

Since 1.9.0 (038) the cards of the carousel overlap: the neighbours of the
centre card at 0.85, each further card 0.02 less, under the one before it,
0.27 of the nominal width in view; the icon of a card, 72 px times the
square root of its scale, lies with its lower third on the thumbnail.

The author, 2026-11-02: the carousel flickers; the overlapping thumbnails do
not look well — put the icon higher, drop the overlap, maybe shrink the
side cards more. The flicker is of the icons: in a frame of a step the live
thumbnail of a card is drawn over its card without holes and covers the
lower third of its own icon, which the frame at rest, with its holes,
shows again — every icon of the eleven in view, on every step.

The variants were tried on the author's desktop on ws2, a build of the
branch on Alt+F11 and Alt+F8 beside the service:

[cols="1,5,3"]
|===
| | Variant | The author

| A | cards in a row, 24 px apart, the icon 8 px above the thumbnail, a card
  a cards from the centre at 0.8^a^ | —
| B | A at 0.7^a^ | "F8 лучше", taken
|===

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `2595884` (1.9.0). Unchanged:
the grid; the size of the icons, the labels under the thumbnail, the
placeholder in the proportions of the monitor (D5 of 038); the selection,
hover and shadow of a card; the steps, their animation, the live
thumbnails; the order of drawing the cards, nearest last (D3 of 038), now
of no consequence.

== Decisions

D1 and D2 the author's, 2026-11-02; D3–D5 made in the specification.

D1 (the author's). *A row.* The cards do not overlap: card j ≥ 1 from the
centre stands with its nominal width — `appearance.thumbnail.width` at its
scale — 24 px beyond that of card j − 1, its card centred in it. A card
between two places moves straight from one to the other.

D2 (the author's). *Shrunk by 0.7 a card.* The card a cards from the centre
is at the scale 0.7^a^: 0.70, 0.49, 0.34, 0.24, 0.17. On ws1 (512 px,
2520 px) nine cards whole, the tenth and eleventh out of view; on 1.9.0
eleven overlapping.

D3. *The icon above the thumbnail.* The icon of a card, of the size of 038,
centred across the card, 8 px above the top of its thumbnail; the flicker
goes with it: no live thumbnail reaches an icon.

D4. *A border of 2 px.* The border of a thumbnail is 2 px wide at any scale.
Until now `drawWindowWithData` divided the 2 px by the scale it draws the
thumbnail at — gg strokes in pixels of the canvas, whatever the matrix —:
2.4 to 2.8 px on the cards of 038, 12 px on a card at 0.17, which left its
live thumbnail a sliver. The labels of the smallest cards keep 2 px off the
thumbnail and off each other, whatever 28 and 34 px times the scale give.

D5. *On whole pixels.* The places of the cards at rest are rounded to whole
pixels; a card in a step moves straight between two of them. At 0.7^a^ the
places are fractional, and the layer of a card drawn apart differed from the
frame by 3 at an edge, against 2 allowed by `TestLayers`.
