== What the test does

`liveAtRest` of `TestLiveAtRest`, for a window with a pixmap of its own
(`own pixmap`) and one drawn into its frame's (`from its frame`):

. a snapshotter (`start`) of the windows listed on a window of the test's
  own, off the screen; the test window captured once;
. a switcher: an overlay never mapped, to which the snapshotter sends its
  ClientMessage; a goroutine reads the ClientMessages and puts each into
  `woken`, a channel of 64; a GL context sharing the snapshotter's;
. `frame`: `BeginFrame`, the window's picture read when newer than the
  last, a fence, `EndFrame`;
. paused, a frame, then `SetLive` with the overlay at 33 ms; the window
  drawn every 5 ms from then on;
. the steps: 42 frames with the window out of view, then 42 with it in
  view, each followed by `time.Sleep(7 ms)` — a frame every 7.5 ms;
. `for len(woken) > 0 { <-woken }` — the drain;
. the rest, 1.5 s: a frame for each wake read, no sooner than 7 ms after
  the one before; 15 pictures at least, and no more frames than pictures
  and one.

== What the snapshotter does

`pkg/snapshot/live.go` of `1a6dbc8`:

* *A wake between two frames.* `publishDone` publishes the passes whose
  fences have signalled and calls `wakeSwitcher`, which sends the
  ClientMessage only when `woken` is false, and sets it: "at most one
  between two frames of the switcher: a switcher at rest blocks on its X
  connection". Only `BeginFrame` sets `woken` false again.
* *The hold.* `passAt`: while frames come one after another — the last two
  ends less than `liveMotion`, 10 ms, apart — a pass due waits until
  `liveHold`, 30 ms, after the last; frames 10 ms apart or more hold
  nothing.
* *The loop's timer.* `liveWait` arms it for the next pass, or the end of
  the hold; `EndFrame` does not wake the loop, `BeginFrame` does when the
  windows shown are another slice (`c70c4e1`). A window changed reports
  one DAMAGE event until a pass subtracts it.

`pkg/ui`: the selector reads the ClientMessage in its loop, `liveEvent`
sets `wanted`; when nothing moves, `liveIdle` presents a frame for it;
every frame, at rest or of an animation, calls `liveBegin`, which takes the
pictures and sets `wanted` false. A wake is answered by the next frame,
whichever it is; none is dropped.

snapshotter, `BeginFrame` (the publications so far, `woken`), a new slice
of windows shown, `EndFrame` (the time since the end before), each turn of
`liveTick` that makes no pass and why — another switcher shown, no window

* *The wake of `c70c4e1`.* The first frame in view of the steps gives
  another slice and wakes the loop; the hold that follows is what keeps a
  pass from the steps.

  drain — which is why the failure needs a host busy enough to hold the
  frame loop of the test back, 3 ms or more at its last frames. With
  `-test.cpu 1` alone it does not fail: what holds the loop back is the
  host, not the number of Go threads.
