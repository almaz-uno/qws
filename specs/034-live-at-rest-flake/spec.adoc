== What and why

`TestLiveAtRest` (`pkg/snapshot/liverest_test.go`, K13 and K14 of 020)
fails now and then: "0 pictures drawn in 1.5s at rest, 0 frames; want 15
at least" — some 1 in 20 runs of `go test ./...` on ws1, of either
subtest, `own pixmap` or `from its frame`, passing when run again (020,
K12; 027, 030 and 031 met it on their commits). Its cause was not found.

What the research found: the test loses a wake of the snapshotter. Its
switcher drains the ClientMessages it has read, once, between the steps and
the rest, and at rest presents a frame only when woken. The snapshotter
wakes the switcher once between two of its frames (`wakeSwitcher`, 020): a
wake read after the last frame of the steps, and drained, leaves it woken,
and no publication after it wakes the switcher again — 0 frames, 0 pictures
in 1.5 s, while the snapshotter passes and publishes the window 43–44 times.
The wake comes before the drain only when a pass is made right after the
last frame of the steps: a pass waits 30 ms after frames that come less
than 10 ms apart (`liveHold`, `liveMotion` of 020), and the steps present
one every 7.5 ms — unless the load holds the test's frame loop back, and
two frames come 10 ms apart or more. Alone on ws1, 1 subtest of 100 failed;
beside the tests of `pkg/carousel` run again and again, 4 of 100; beside 24
goroutines spinning, 24 of 160 (research).

The snapshotter does what 020 says, and the switcher of qws answers every
wake with a frame — `liveEvent` marks a picture wanted, the next frame's
`liveBegin` takes it — so qws does not meet this. The test is wrong: the
change makes its switcher answer the wakes as qws's does, and changes no
code of qws.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `1a6dbc8` (1.6.0). Unchanged:
every file but `pkg/snapshot/liverest_test.go` — the snapshotter, its
passes, its wakes and their schedule (020), the pause for the switchers of
other instances (011), the snapshots (008, 016); in the test, its windows,
the window drawn every 5 ms, the steps out of view and back, the rest of
1.5 s at an interval of 33 ms, and what it checks: 15 pictures at least, no
more frames than pictures and one.

== Decisions

Made on the way, 2026-10-08, for the author to confirm:

* *The test, not the snapshotter.* The snapshotter could wake the switcher
  again when no frame follows a wake — after a time, or at every
  publication — but no switcher of qws drops a wake: it would send
  ClientMessages, and qws would present frames, for nothing, against the
  one wake between two frames of 020.
* *The switcher of the test answers the wakes as qws's does.* A frame of
  the test answers the wakes read before it: `frame` drains them before
  `BeginFrame`, as `liveBegin` takes the picture `liveEvent` marked wanted.
  The drain between the steps and the rest goes: a wake read after the last
  frame of the steps is answered at rest. Rejected: a frame presented after
  the drain, before the rest — it serves as well, but is a frame qws does
  not present.
* *The bounds as they are.* A wake sent before a frame and read after its
  drain is answered by a frame more, which draws no new picture — qws does
  the same. At rest it cannot happen: a frame follows the wake it answers,
  and the snapshotter sends none in between. It can once, at the start of
  the rest, for a wake sent before the last frame of the steps: the bound
  of one frame more than pictures stays.

== Behavior

. *The frame of the test.* Before it takes the pictures (`BeginFrame`), a
  frame drains the wakes the test has read: they are answered by it, the
  pictures published before them among those it takes.
. *The rest.* It starts right after the steps, with the wakes read since
  their last frame: a frame for each wake, no sooner than 7 ms after the
  frame before, as before.
