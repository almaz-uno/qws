== What and why

In the carousel the selected window is the card in the middle, where the
eye already is. A switch to the grid — the layout key `q` (specification
026) or `g` — puts it in one tile of up to 36, anywhere in the window,
marked only by the selection frame of the grid, which appears with the grid
at once: the eye has to search for it. The author, 2026-10-04: after a
switch to the grid, an explicit animation of the selected window, so that
the eye sees it at once.

What a switch does now (research): the layers are dropped, the grid's frame
is drawn on the CPU and presented — 62–74 ms of drawing and 7–22 ms of
presenting, 76–97 ms in all, at six switches with 33 windows on E1 — and
only then are the grid's layers drawn in the background. Until they are there nothing of the grid moves: a step
is made at once (`stepTo`), and so would an animation of the selection.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `c3c598e` (1.4.1). Unchanged:
the pictures at rest of both layouts and of both renderers — the frames of
`cpu` and the scene set Σ with its digests; the carousel; the grid's steps,
hover and mouse; the live thumbnails of 020 at rest.

== Decisions

D1–D4 decided by the author on 2026-10-04, each the option recommended;
D5 and D6 made in the specification and taken with them.

D1. *The selection frame converges onto the tile.* After the switch the
selection frame of the grid and the shadow of the selected tile appear
scaled up about the tile's centre by `locate_zoom` and transparent, and
within `locate_duration` shrink onto the tile and become opaque, eased out
as the other animations (007): a motion from all sides toward the tile,
which the eye catches wherever it is, ending as the frame of the grid is at
rest. Through the layers and the scale the hover frame zooms with
(`zoomRect`, 010 and 014); no new layer.
Rejected:
(b) a pulse — the frame and the shadow grow to some 1.15 and back, twice:
little motion, near the tile only, a small change the eye still has to
find;
(c) a flight — the selected card moves from the middle of the carousel to
its tile while the grid fades in: the eye follows it from where it was, but
it needs the layers of both layouts at once, a cross-fade of the two and
the card's picture at both sizes — several times the work of (a), and
nothing of the grid could move until all of it is drawn.

D2. *When.* After a switch to the grid, by the layout key or by `g`. Not
when an activation opens in the grid: the overlay then has its own
appearance, the fade and zoom of `show`, and the eye no place to come from.
Not after a switch to the carousel, whose selection is in the middle.
Alternative: also when an activation opens in the grid, after its fade-in.

D3. *At once.* While the carousel is shown, the grid's layers are drawn in
the background — after the carousel's own, at the priority of the layers
of the next step — and kept beside the carousel's: a switch shows the grid
from its layers in the next frame, without the frame of the CPU, and the
frame converges from that frame. A switch to the carousel the same way,
its layers kept. The cost: the layers of the grid drawn once an
activation, a tiles layer of at most the window's size — up to some 14 MB
of GPU memory on E1 (2520×1400×4 bytes) — while they were drawn only after a switch;
whatever drops the layers now (the window list, the thumbnails, the
workspace filter) drops both.
Alternative: as now, the grid drawn on the CPU and its layers after it, the
frame converging from when they are there — some 140 ms after the key on
E1, an estimate to be measured (research, T1).

D4. *Two keys,* as the hover has (`hover_duration`, `hover_zoom`, 010 and
014): `appearance.animation.locate_duration`, 400 ms by default — longer
than a step, 150 ms by the author's `duration`, as it is to be seen, not
only followed; `0s`: no animation, the grid as now. And
`appearance.animation.locate_zoom`, 1.6 by default: the scale the frame
converges from; a factor not above 1 gives a warning and the default is
used. Both to be tried in the author's trial (K9).

D5. *Not under `cpu`*, nor with `appearance.animation.enabled: false`: the
grid as now, like every animation of 007.

D6. *Keys during the animation.* A step — an arrow, the main key — ends the
animation at once, and the step moves the selection frame from the tile, as
now; a switch back to the carousel ends it; the hover of another tile does
not. The live thumbnails make no pass while it runs, frames coming one
after another (020), and start 30 ms after it ends.

== Behavior

After `q` or `g` from the carousel, with `glx` and animations on: the grid
in the next frame, its selection frame and the shadow of the selected tile
at `locate_zoom` and transparent; within `locate_duration` they shrink onto
the tile and become opaque; then the grid at rest as now. Nothing else of
the grid moves. With the grid's layers not yet drawn — a switch right after
the activation opens — the grid as now, the animation from when they are
there if within `locate_duration` of the key, else none.

== Metrics

L:: time from the key of a switch to the presenting of the first frame of
the grid: `activation_ms` of the record "Frame" of cause `key` against the
time of the key; and to the first frame of the animation.
F:: the frames of the animation: the share of intervals above 1.5 refresh
periods and the p95 of the intervals, from the records "Animation frame" of
kind `locate`, against those of the fade-ins of the same runs.
