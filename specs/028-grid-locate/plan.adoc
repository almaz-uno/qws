== Approach

*The layers of both layouts* (D3, D7). The layer cache holds the layers of
the carousel and of the grid side by side, as it can: their keys differ
already. The base — the canvas with the header — differs by the hint of the
layout key, so each layout gets one, `carouselBase` and `gridBase` among the
keys of the cache in place of the fixed layer 1: the base of the layout
shown drawn on the spot when it is missing, as now (`ensureBase`), the
other's in the background. `prefetch` asks, after the layers of the layout
shown, for the other layout's at rest: in the carousel, the grid's base and
`gridLayers`; in the grid, the carousel's base and its cards at rest at the
selection (`motionLayers(sel, sel)`), anew after each step of the grid.
`carouselReady` and `gridReady` say whether a layout's layers are held,
whichever is shown.

`switchLayout` no longer drops the layers. With the layers of the layout
switched to held, it ends what moves (`cancelStep`), presents a scene of
them at once — of the grid, at the start of the animation when it runs —
and draws the frame at rest of that layout in the background, presented
when it comes or at the end of the animation; during the fade-in, the
fade's frames present the scene. Without them, as now: the frame drawn on
the CPU, the layers asked for. `dropLayers` stays where it is — a new
activation, for the window list, the thumbnails and the geometry, and the
workspace filter — and drops both layouts' layers. A frame at rest drawn in
the background carries its layout, so that one of the carousel is not taken
for the grid's after a switch.

*The animation* (D1, D2, D5, D6). `locateAnimation`, `pkg/ui/locate.go`: a
level from 0 to 1 in `locate_duration` along ease-out cubic (`motion`),
begun by a switch to the grid with its layers held — or, without them, when
they come, if within `locate_duration` of the key. `gridItems` places the
shadow of the selected tile and the selection frame through
`effects{fade, zoom}.look(v, locate_zoom)` about the tile's centre with
`zoomRect`, as the hover frames; every other item as at rest. The loop
presents its frames as those of a step or a hover: the scene while it
moves, then the frame at rest, or the scene at rest until that comes. A
step (`stepTo`, `cancelStep`) and a switch end it, a hover does not;
`liveDue` is false while it runs, and `handleEventsSync` makes frames while
anything moves (`moving`). Under `cpu`, with the animations off or at
`locate_duration: 0s`, none.

*The keys* (D4). `appearance.animation.locate_duration` and `locate_zoom`
as `hover_duration` and `hover_zoom` (014): `Default()`, `setDefaults`,
flags, variables. `parseAnimation` reports a negative duration and takes it
as 0, and a factor not above 1, taking the default.

*The records.* The first frame of a switch presented from the layers is a
record "Frame" of cause `key` — `total_ms`, the time from the key, is L —
and, with the animation, also its first "Animation frame" of kind
`locate`, whose `response_ms` is the time from the key to the first frame
of the animation; when the animation waits for the layers, that record
alone. Each frame of the animation is a record "Animation frame" of kind
`locate` with its `interval_ms`, for F. "Switching layout" says whether the
layers of the layout switched to were held (`layers`).

*The end of an activation.* `appearance.layout` is restored when the overlay
is unmapped (`hide`), not when the event that ends the activation is read:
the fade-out then shows the layout shown — with D3, the frame the presenter
holds may be the other layout's until the frame at rest of the layout
switched to comes. The next activation opens in `appearance.layout` as
before (026, D2).
