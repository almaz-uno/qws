== What and why

Three things of the picture are constants of qws that earlier
specifications left open as keys:

* the header of specification 005 — the hostname and the version at the top
  left of the overlay — is always drawn; 005 leaves open "a configuration key
  to hide the header, if it turns out to be in the way";
* the hover frame of specification 010 moves in the duration of every
  animation; 010 leaves open "a duration of its own for the hover, should
  one for all be too slow for it";
* the zoom of the appearance and the disappearance starts from 0.92 of the
  size of the overlay, that of the hover frame from 1.05 of its size, both
  constants of `pkg/ui`; 010 leaves open "the zoom factors as keys, should
  the constants not suit".

The change makes them four keys whose defaults are the constants of today.
They are one specification because they are alike: a constant becomes a
key, the constant its default, and a configuration without the keys is read
as before.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `f90abc7` (1.2.0). Unchanged:
with the defaults, the picture of both renderers byte for byte — the frames
of `cpu`, those of `glx` at rest — and the animations of `glx`; every other
key; how keys are read from the file, the environment and the flags.

== Decisions

Made on the way, 2026-10-01, for the author to confirm:

* *The header is a section of its own*, `appearance.header.enabled`, as
  `window_background.enabled` and `animation.enabled`: the header is a part
  of the picture, not of the animation. Off, it hides the hostname and the
  version together — one key for the one header — and the grid takes the
  whole window, as before 005.
* *The selector applies it*: with the header off it gives the renderer no
  hostname and no version, and the renderer already draws no header without
  them (005, "Behavior"). The frame is then that of the scenes of
  specification 001, which have no header, so no scene of its own is added:
  its digest would repeat theirs.
* *`hover_duration` defaults to 0, "that of `duration`"*. A default of
  150 ms would detach the hover from `duration` in a configuration that sets
  only `duration`, and change its animation; 0 keeps it. Here 0 does not mean
  "at once", as it does for `duration`: the hover at once is `hover: none`,
  a key of 010. `enabled: false` and `duration: 0` still stop every motion,
  the hover's too: they turn the animation off as a whole (010).
* *One factor for the overlay, one for the hover*, `overlay_zoom` and
  `hover_zoom`, as there were two constants: the appearance starts from the
  factor the disappearance ends at. A factor of their own for `show` and
  `hide` was not asked for.
* *A factor is a positive number*: above 1 the overlay closes in, below 1
  the hover frame grows out of its tile — the way 010 decided against for
  the hover, left to the user. Anything else — 0, a negative number, NaN,
  infinity — gives a warning and the default is used: at 0 the present pass
  of `glx` would divide by the scale.
  A negative `hover_duration` gives a warning and is 0, as a negative
  `duration` of 010 is. Both are checked where 010 checks its keys, when the
  selector is created.

Two decisions of 010, made for the author to confirm, are thereby replaced:
"one duration for every animation" and "the factors are constants, not
keys".

== Behavior

=== Keys

[cols="2,1,5"]
|===
| Key | Default | Meaning

| `appearance.header.enabled`
| `true`
| The header of specification 005. `false`: no hostname and no version are
  drawn, and the grid lays its tiles over the whole window.

| `appearance.animation.hover_duration`
| `0s`
| The time of the hover animation; `0`: that of `duration`.

| `appearance.animation.overlay_zoom`
| `0.92`
| The scale of its size the overlay zooms from as it appears, with `zoom` in
  `show`, and to as it disappears, with `zoom` in `hide`.

| `appearance.animation.hover_zoom`
| `1.05`
| The scale of its size the hover frame zooms from as it comes, and to as it
  goes, with `zoom` in `hover`.
|===

Environment variables and flags are named as in specification 002:
`QWS_APPEARANCE_HEADER_ENABLED`, `--appearance-header-enabled`,
`QWS_APPEARANCE_ANIMATION_HOVER_DURATION`,
`--appearance-animation-hover-duration` and so on.

`appearance.animation.duration` becomes the time of every animation but the
hover's when `hover_duration` is set; the step, the appearance and the
disappearance keep it.

=== Existing configurations

No key, environment variable or flag is renamed or removed, and no default
changes. A configuration without the four keys is read as by 1.2.0 and
shows its picture. New keys make the release a minor one (constitution,
"Releases and versions").

  `TestGLXPresenterMatchesCPU` pass with `pkg/carousel/testdata/scenes.json`

  150 ms for the step, the fades and the hover, 0.92 and 1.05
  (`TestParseAnimation`).

| K3
| Hover duration: at 0 the hover takes `duration`; at 80 ms its own, while
  the step and the fades keep `duration`; a negative one gives one warning
  and is that of `duration`; with `enabled: false` or `duration: 0` nothing
  moves; the levels of the hover move in its duration
  (`TestHoverDuration` in `pkg/ui`).

| K4
| Zoom factors: 0.5 and 1.5 are the scales the overlay and the hover frame
  start from, and the disappearance is at 0.75 half way; 0, −1, NaN and
  infinity give the defaults and a warning per key (`TestZoomFactors` in
  `pkg/ui`).

| K5
| Sources: the defaults, each key from a file and from its `QWS_` variable
  over the file (`TestAppearanceKeys` in `internal/config`), and from its
  flag (`TestAppearanceFlags` in `cmd/qws`); the round trip of K3 of 002
  with the new keys (`TestRoundTrip`, `TestTagNames`).

| K6
| `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...` and `make check-specs` pass.
|===
