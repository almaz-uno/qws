== Approach

*The key* (D2). `Animation.Enabled` becomes a string, `auto` in
`Default()` — so `config init` writes `enabled: auto` — and in
`setDefaults`. `Animation.Mode()` reads it: `auto`; `true` and `false`,
`on` and `off`, and whatever `strconv.ParseBool` reads — `1`, `t`, `TRUE`
— as `true` and `false`, the case aside, so that a variable or a flag
that the bool took before means what it meant; anything else is `auto`,
with a warning that `parseAnimation` reports with the others. A file that
says `enabled: true` or `false` holds a YAML bool, which the weak decoding
of viper's `Unmarshal` turns into `"1"` or `"0"`: `Load` writes it back as
`true` or `false` when the value read is a bool, so that `config show`
prints what the file says. The flag `--appearance-animation-enabled`
becomes a string flag of default `auto` whose `NoOptDefVal` is `true`:
alone it is `true`, as before, and `--appearance-animation-enabled=false`
is `false`, as before. `QWS_APPEARANCE_ANIMATION_ENABLED` is read as the
file is.

`appearance.animation.vnc_ports`, a list of ports, `[5900]`: in
`Default()` and `setDefaults` — its variable the ports joined by commas —
and the flag `--appearance-animation-vnc-ports`. An empty list, which
checks nothing, is written in the file, `vnc_ports: []`: an empty variable
is unset to viper, and an int-slice flag takes no empty value. A port
outside 1–65535 is reported and left out.

*Animated or still* (D1, D3). The selector keeps the options of the
configuration, `animated`, and those of the activation, `anim`: the same,
or none — the zero `animationOptions`, which `enabled: false` gives now —
while still. `setStill` sets them, with the durations of the motions of
the step and of the fade, as `NewSelector` does now. `false`:
`parseAnimation` gives none, as now; `true`: the options, always; `auto`:
`autoAnimation` decides, on a presenter that composes — under `cpu`
nothing moves and nothing is checked. While still, `setLive(true)` does
nothing: no live pass.

*The start of an activation* (D4, D6). `Show`, once it has read the
refresh period and before the first frame — of a quick press too — asks
`autoAnimation.begin`. A viewer — a line of `/proc/net/tcp` or
`/proc/net/tcp6` in state `01` on a local port of `vnc_ports` — makes the
activation still. Else the frames: still within five minutes of the time
they slipped; past five minutes the activation is animated and its count
starts anew — the probe, which a connected viewer defers: it says when it
is gone. The first frame and its fade-in follow from `anim` as set
(`showFirst`, out of `Show`, for the tests). The clock of the probe is a
function of `autoAnimation`, which the tests replace; so is the check for
a viewer.

*The count* (D5, O1). `logAnimationFrame` returns the interval it logs, 0
for the first frame of an animation. `frame`, after a frame presented,
takes the intervals of its records of a step, of the hover and of the
convergence, and counts the frame once, at the longest; not the fades
(D5, O1); not the frame at rest that `uploadIdle` presents after its step
has ended, whose interval is the time of its drawing, not a refresh the
display missed. A frame is late above 1.25 refresh periods; a ring of the
last 60 frames counted runs on across activations; the third late in it
makes it still at once: `stillNow` — the options none; the motions of the
step, of the hover levels and of the convergence, and a fade-in, at their
targets in the next frame, their duration 0; a fade-out ended there, the
overlay unmapped; the live passes off. While still, under `true` and under
`false` nothing is counted. `countsSlips` names the kinds counted, for
`frame` and for the replay of the batches.

*The records* (D7). `autoAnimation` keeps why the activation is still — a
viewer, the frames, or not — and logs at info level when that changes:
"Animation still: a VNC viewer connected" with the port, "Animation
still: the frames slip" with the late frames and the frames counted,
"Animation back". The check for a viewer is a debug record with its time.

*The batches* (K2). The records "Animation frame" of `vnc-A.txt` and
`vnc-B.txt` reduced to what the count needs — the activation, the kind and
the interval of each record that has one — in `pkg/ui/testdata`, by a
command written in the research; `TestSlipReplay` replays them into
`autoAnimation` as `frame` would.

== Files

|===
| File | What changes

| `internal/config/config.go`
| `Enabled` a string, `auto`; `VNCPorts` at the end of `Animation`; `Mode`;
  the YAML bool written back in `Load`; `setDefaults`.

| `cmd/qws/root.go`
| `--appearance-animation-enabled` a string flag, alone `true`;
  `--appearance-animation-vnc-ports`; `applyFlags`.

| `parseAnimation` by `Mode`, its warning.

| `pkg/ui/auto.go`
| New: `autoAnimation` — the start of an activation, the count, the probe,
  the records —, `countsSlips`, the ports read; the selector's
  `initAnimations`, `beginAnimation`, `setStill` and `stillNow`.

| `pkg/ui/viewer.go`
| New: the connections established on the ports, from `/proc/net/tcp` and
  `/proc/net/tcp6`.

| `pkg/ui/selector.go`
| `animated`, `still`, `auto`; `initAnimations` in `NewSelector`, with the
  keys in the record "Animations"; `beginAnimation` and `showFirst` in
  `Show`.

| `pkg/ui/animation.go`, `pkg/ui/timing.go`, `pkg/ui/live.go`
| The frames counted in `frame`; the interval returned by
  `logAnimationFrame`; no live pass while still.

| `pkg/ui/auto_test.go`, `pkg/ui/viewer_test.go`, `pkg/ui/testdata/`
| K2–K4; the batches.

| `internal/config/config_test.go`, `cmd/qws/root_test.go`,
  `pkg/ui/effects_test.go`
| K1; the tests of 010 and 028 with the string.

| `doc/configuration.asciidoc`, `config.yaml.example`, `README.md`
| The key, its values and `vnc_ports`; auto.
|===
