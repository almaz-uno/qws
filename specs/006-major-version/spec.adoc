== What and why

Section "Releases and versions" of the constitution, introduced by
specification 004, says when the minor and the patch version change, not the
major one. The author asked to take over the rules of siphon, whose
constitution has the rule (its commit `cbbd36f`, 2026-09-30), and plans
1.0.0 after the animation (2026-10-01).

Unchanged: the program, the other rules of the section, the release
process.

== Constitution

Section "Releases and versions" gets, after the rule on minor and patch
versions:

* The major version when a release is incompatible with the previous ones —
  a configuration they read is not read the same way, a key, a flag or an
  environment variable is removed or changes its meaning without an alias —
  but always by agreement with the author: without it such a change goes out
  as a minor version. Or by the author's direct request: 1.0.0, planned
  after the animation (2026-10-01).

The rule is siphon's, with qws's kinds of incompatibility in place of
siphon's nodes, protocol and database. A major release, `X.0.0`, is a chapter
of `RELEASE-NOTES.adoc` like a minor one.
