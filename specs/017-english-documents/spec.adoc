== What and why

Section "Language" of the constitution makes English the language of the
documentation (`AGENTS.md`), and its open question "Non-English documents"
names four documents that are not in it: they are either translated or the
rule gets an exception. On `develop` at `f90abc7`, the lines with Cyrillic
are:

[cols="3,1,1"]
|===
| File | Lines with Cyrillic | Lines

| `doc/optimization.asciidoc` | 307 | 770
| `doc/font-fallback.md` | 29 | 85
| `doc/windows-switcher.asciidoc` | 4 | 2669
| `doc/configuration.asciidoc` | 1 | 1153
| `pkg/carousel/scenes_test.go` | 5 | 337
|===

The four lines of `doc/windows-switcher.asciidoc` are a heading, a sentence
and two code comments; the line of `doc/configuration.asciidoc` is a sentence
on font fallback. `pkg/carousel/scenes_test.go` is not a document: its five
lines are window titles, the input of a test.

The change:

. translates the four documents;
. leaves the titles of `pkg/carousel/scenes_test.go` as they are, and the
  constitution says why;
. closes the open question.

Unchanged: the program and its tests; what the documents say, stale or not
(section "Open decisions"); their structure, markup, code, identifiers,
commands, paths, numbers, units and links; the other documents.

== Decisions

* *Translated, no exception.* The constitution names these documents as the
  description of behavior and the record of design decisions (section
  "Documentation is part of the change, not its consequence"), and decisions
  are made from them; a part of them a reader of English cannot read is a
  part nobody checks against the code. Specification 001 already had to take
  over the content of a document in Russian instead of merging it
  (`research.adoc`, "Prototype"). An exception would make the rule of
  "Language" a list of files.
* *Line for line.* Every line with Cyrillic is replaced by its translation,
  and no other line changes. The number of lines stays, the diff reads as
  pairs of an original and its translation, and another change of the same
  document merges unless it edits one of those lines.
* *A translation, not a revision.* A passage keeps what it says, also where
  it no longer matches the code: a revision is a decision of its own, and
  inside a translation it could not be reviewed as one. What was found stale
  is listed in "Open decisions". One word is not kept: `doc/font-fallback.md`
  called `MultiFallbackFace` a class in one sentence and a type in another,
  and the translation says type in both.
* *Prose and comments.* Prose, headings, table cells and the comments in code
  blocks are translated; identifiers, commands, paths, outputs, numbers and
  units are kept, as are the emoji marks of the status table. The register
  is that of `specs/`.
* *Test data stays.* The titles of `portableWindows` and `hostWindows` in
  `pkg/carousel/scenes_test.go` are what scene set Σ of specification 001 is
  drawn with: the SHA-256 of every frame is recorded in
  `pkg/carousel/testdata/scenes.json`. The portable titles draw Cyrillic
  with the Go font alone; the host ones mix it with glyphs only DejaVu Sans
  has, so that the fallback changes fonts within a title. Translated, they
  would change the frames and the digests and drop the Cyrillic the test is
  there for. Such strings are not text written for a reader.

== Constitution

Section "Language" gets, after its rule:

[quote]
____
Strings a test feeds to the program are data, not text for a reader, and keep
their language: the Cyrillic window titles of `pkg/carousel/scenes_test.go`,
on which font fallback is checked (specification `017-english-documents`).
____

The open question "Non-English documents" is removed.

== Acceptance criteria

The checks run in the root of the repository. Lines with Cyrillic, per
tracked file:

[source,bash]
----
git ls-files | while read f; do
	n=$(grep -cP '[\x{0400}-\x{04FF}]' "$f" 2>/dev/null)
	[ "${n:-0}" -gt 0 ] && echo "$n $f"
done
----

The shape of a line: its leading characters that are neither letters nor
backquotes — indentation, heading and list marks, table bars, comment marks —
and its counts of backquotes, of `**` and of bars:

[source,bash]
----
shape() { perl -CSD -ne 'chomp; my ($p) = /^([^\p{L}`]*)/; my $b = () = /`/g;
	my $s = () = /\*\*/g; my $u = () = /\|/g; print "$p\t$b\t$s\t$u\n"'; }
for f in doc/optimization.asciidoc doc/font-fallback.md \
	doc/windows-switcher.asciidoc doc/configuration.asciidoc; do
	diff <(git show f90abc7:"$f" | shape) <(shape < "$f") || echo "$f differs"
done
----

== Open decisions

Found stale while translating, and translated as they stand. Whether each
document is revised or deleted is a decision of its own (constitution,
"Documentation is part of the change, not its consequence").

`doc/optimization.asciidoc`, written 2026-01-23, before specifications 001,
007 and 008:

* "Two backends — CPU (`renderer-cpu.go`) and OpenGL
  (`renderer-opengl.go`)": `renderer-opengl.go` was removed by specification
  001 (`538e5c8`). The backends are `cpu` and `glx`, the default; frames are
  always drawn by the CPU renderer, and `glx` presents them through GLX,
  without `PutImage` (`pkg/carousel/factory.go`). Output through `PutImage`,
  and optimization #3, XSHM for output, concern `cpu` alone.
* Optimization #4: `DrawImage()` still ends in `Sync()`, but its strips are
  checked after all of them are sent — one round trip, not one per strip
  (`pkg/carousel/window.go`).
* Optimization #1, doubtful: the thumbnails of `win.Preview` are captured
  scaled, at most 512×512, and kept between activations — by the snapshots
  of `pkg/snapshot` (specification 008) and the cache of `focus.Watcher`
  (`cmd/qws/root.go`); what every frame redoes is the resampling to the size
  of the tile (`pkg/carousel/prepared.go`).
* Optimization #2, "not implemented": a `sync.Pool` recycles the scratch
  pixels of prepared images (`pkg/carousel/prepared.go`), not the frame.
* "The collected profiles are in `profiles/`", and
  `profiles/cpu_20260119_152345.prof` with its pair: the directory is ignored
  by git, and the files are not in the repository.
* "Before optimization (estimate)": the figures have no method or conditions
  (constitution, "Decisions rest on measurements"); the measured ones are in
  specification 001. The timing proposed under "Methods of measurement"
  exists in another form: `pkg/ui/timing.go` logs the latency of a frame at
  debug level.
* "Not recommended: CGO-based libraries — complicate the build": the build
  needs cgo since specification 001 (GLX, `go-gl/gl`). "Current
  dependencies" names three of the ten direct ones of `go.mod`, and lists
  `zerolog`, in use, under "Recommended additions".

`doc/font-fallback.md`, written 2026-01-13:

* Its links `pkg/carousel/...` are relative to `doc/` and resolve to
  `doc/pkg/carousel/...`, which does not exist.
* "Usage": the type is used in `pkg/carousel/renderer-cpu.go`, not
  `renderer.go`, through `Config.face`, which takes the face from a
  `FontSet` or the shared cache (specification 007); `Close()` does nothing.

Specification 001 removed `renderer-opengl.go` and left
`doc/optimization.asciidoc` describing it, while the constitution has a
document that no longer matches the code updated or deleted in the same
change; the revision would close that gap as well.
