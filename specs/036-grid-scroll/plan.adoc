== Amendments to the specification

Made here on the figures of the research and written into the
specification in the same change set:

* D1: the least is at most what `appearance.thumbnail` lets a tile be — a
  box of (width + 20) × height. With the defaults, 256×256, a least of 320
  could not be had, and every grid would scroll. 320 on E1, 276 with the
  defaults (research, "The least thumbnail").
* D3, K1: 6 columns on E1, not 7: 7 columns give thumbnails of 317 px
  (research, "The columns").
* D9: one layer of all the rows (research, "The layer of all the rows");
  the open decision dropped.

== Approach

*The layout* (D1–D3, `pkg/carousel/renderer-cpu.go`). `Config` gets
`GridMinThumbWidth`, the least m, and `GridScroll`, the scroll of the view
in whole pixels. `layoutGrid` computes the least m′ of D1 and the columns
of D3 — `GridColumns` with its bound of 6 when m is 0, of cfit otherwise
—, lays the grid out as `develop` does and keeps it if its thumbnail boxes
hold m′ × m′·H/W, or m is 0; otherwise lays the rows out from the band of
the header down, the tiles of the research, "The grid that scrolls". The
layout keeps the view: its top, the band in whole pixels up; the largest
scroll; the scroll of `cfg`, within it. `tile(i)` is the tile at that
scroll. A grid that fits has a largest scroll of 0 and is laid out, and
drawn, as in `develop`.

*The view, for `pkg/ui`.* `GridView(n, cfg)`: the top of the view, the
largest scroll, the distance of the rows. `GridFollow(n, i, cfg)`: the
least scroll from `cfg.GridScroll` that brings the row of tile i in whole,
with the spacing around it, within the ends (D4). `GridTile` is the tile
at `cfg.GridScroll`, as before at 0.

*The frame* (D6). `DrawGridLayout` draws the canvas, the header and, for a
grid that scrolls, the track of the scrollbar; keeps the rows of the band
of the header; draws the tiles at the scroll, those out of the view left
out; writes the rows kept back over what the tiles drew under the header;
draws the thumb of the scrollbar. The scrollbar is 4 px wide, 4 px from the
right edge of the grid, from the top of the view to the bottom of the
window less the spacing; its thumb the view's share of the content, at
least 24 px, at the scroll's share of the largest, in whole pixels.

*The layers* (D9, `pkg/carousel/layers.go`). `GridTiles` draws the tiles
of all the rows at scroll 0 on a canvas of the content and crops it; none
for a content taller than 16384 px. `GridBase` is `CarouselBase` with the
track; `GridScrollThumb` the thumb at scroll 0, and `GridThumbOffset` how
far the scroll moves it. `SceneItem.Clip`, when not empty, cuts the quad of
the item (`present_glx_scene.go`). The base of the grid is `GridBase`; the
thumb a layer among the grid's, held as none when the grid does not
scroll.

*The scene* (`pkg/ui/animation.go`). The scroll of a scene is that of the
view at now, rounded to a pixel: `s.config.GridScroll` at rest, the motion
`step.sy` while it moves. The tiles layer is placed at minus the scroll,
the shadows and frames at their tiles in the rows less it — `step.gx` and
`step.gy` are in the coordinates of the rows —, the thumb at its offset;
for a grid that scrolls each item is cut to the view.

*The scroll* (D4, D5, D7). `stepTo` takes the scroll of `GridFollow` for
its target: at once with the selection, or moved by `step.sy` beside
`step.pos`, of its duration — 0 while still (031). A step moves while
either moves. The wheel, button 4 or 5 in the grid, scrolls by a row
within the ends through the same step, the selection where it is, its
frame at rest drawn for the event; then the hover is found anew at the
pointer. The view follows the selection at the start of an activation, at
a switch to the grid and when the workspace filter changes the windows.
Up and Down as in 026: around a column is a wrap, at once. The frame at
rest drawn in the background carries its scroll and is not taken for
another.

*The mouse* (D5). `getWindowIndexAtPositionGrid` takes the tiles at the
scroll and no point above the view.

*The live thumbnails* (D8, `pkg/carousel/live.go`, `pkg/ui/live.go`).
`GridLive` gives the items of all the rows at scroll 0, cut to the
content; `GridLiveView` moves them by a scroll and cuts them, and their
holes, to the view. The selector keeps the items of the rows by the
generation of its layers and views them at the scroll of each frame. The
windows the snapshotter passes are those of the tiles in the view at
rest, whole or cut.

*The key* (K5). `appearance.grid.min_thumbnail_width`, `int`, 320 in
`Default()` and `setDefaults` — so the variable is read —, the flag
`--appearance-grid-min-thumbnail-width`; `Grid.MinThumbWidth()` gives the
value and a warning for a negative one, which takes the default, logged by
`NewSelector`. Existing configurations have no such key: they get 320, and
a grid that does not fit at it scrolls; 0 keeps the grid of 1.6.1.
