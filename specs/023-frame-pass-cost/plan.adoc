The plan of the options recommended in the specification — D1 b, D2 c, D3,
and a for D4–D7; should the author choose others, it is written again. It
starts from `develop` once pull request #60 (020) is merged: the code it
changes is 020's.

== Approach

*The chain.* A type of `pkg/snapshot/xrender.go`, `chain`: the copy of the
window — a pixmap of its size and of the depth of the pixmap it is taken
from — and its graphics context, made with graphics exposures off; the
source picture in that pixmap's visual, padded; a pixmap a8r8g8b8 and its
picture for each halving; the picture it scales into, given; the window's
size and the visual and depth of the pixmap; whether it copies. `newChain`
makes it and sets the transform and the filter of each picture once, as
the loop of `scale` sets them now. `run(pixmap, at)` sends the copy from
`at` — none for a window at the corner of its pixmap, whose source picture
is then that pixmap's — and the composites, and returns their cookies.
`free` frees the pictures, then the pixmaps and the graphics context.

`scale` becomes `newChain`, `run` and `free`, so that the snapshots of 018
and 022 keep their pictures and their requests but for their order and the
graphics exposures of the copy: the chain made before the copy, not around
it; the X server draws the same pixels.

*A pass from the frame.* `scaleFromFrame` of `live.go` takes the window's
chain — made at its first pass of an activation, and anew when the window's
size, the visual or the depth of its pixmap, whether it copies, or, without
a copy, the pixmap itself differs — runs it at the offset kept, and sends
`TranslateCoordinates` of the window to the frame. Its reply marks the
composites' cookies done, so their `Check` sends no `GetInputFocus`; the
offset it tells, plus the frame's border, is compared with the one used:
another, the chain run again at it, once — made anew if the window has come
to or left the corner of the pixmap — and the offset kept; another again,
the pass not made and the window left changed. A failed composite or reply
leaves the window stale, as in 020.

*Where the window lies.* `window` keeps `at`, the offset last told, and
`viaBorder`, the frame's border: `namePixmap` keeps the border of the
`GetGeometry` it sends the frame, and `pixmapRect` of a snapshot, which asks
as now, keeps the offset it gets.

*Freed.* `setLive` frees the chains of every window when the live passes
end (`want.overlay == 0`), `forget` with the window; `dropScaled` frees the
scaled pixmap as now.

*The counting.* `TestFramePassRequests` counts R by the sequence numbers of
xgb's cookies (`xgb.Cookie.Sequence`) around a pass on the snapshotter of
the tests (`frameSnapshotter`), which runs no loop: nothing else sends on
its connection, and the test polls the connection's events
(`PollForEvent`) for NoExposure. Written before the change and run on 020's
code, it fails there and gives the counts of K3.

*The pictures.* `TestFramePassPictures` draws its windows anew between
passes by copying into them, in the X server, one of two pixmaps of the
test, so that 200 passes of a window of 2556×1357 take seconds, not the
uploads of a picture each; the snapshot of each picture is taken once and
compared with every live picture of it.

*The benchmark.* `BenchmarkLivePassFrame` takes the window's size from
`LIVE_FRAME_SIZE`, `2556x1357` by default.

No document of behavior changes: nothing the user sees does
(constitution, "Documentation is part of the change").

== Preserved

The pictures of the passes from the frame and of the snapshots, byte for
byte; the scaled pixmap of 020, its binding, `glXWaitX`, `Rebind` and the
copy into the live texture; the schedule of 020; the passes of the windows
with a pixmap of their own; the frame's pixmap never bound on the GPU; one
pass of a window at a time — a window with a pass under way takes no other
(`livePassable` of 020) — so a chain is never run by two passes at once.

== Order

The counting and the benchmark's size on 020's code, and S5x before any
code: the references. Then D3, D1 and D2, each with its tests; the
benchmarks; S5x with the change. See `tasks.adoc`.
