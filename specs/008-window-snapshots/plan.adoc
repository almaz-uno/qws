== Approach

A snapshotter of its own replaces the snapshots of the focus watcher. It
has its own X connection, so that the event loop of the switcher, which
reads every event while the switcher is shown, does not take its events;
and its own GL context, current on a thread of its own, on a pbuffer.

It follows the client windows: `_NET_CLIENT_LIST` on the root, and per
window its structure (map, unmap, size, destruction) and a DAMAGE object
reporting the first change after each subtraction. A window that becomes
viewable, or changes size, has its pixmap named and bound anew; a change
marks it dirty. A schedule captures each dirty viewable window once its
last snapshot is `snapshot_interval` old, a window that became viewable
after 100 ms, and, when the switcher asks at an activation, every dirty
viewable window at once.

A capture releases and binds the texture of the pixmap, draws the area
average into a framebuffer of the thumbnail size, and reads it back. The
thumbnails are kept by window, as the watcher keeps them now.

The focus watcher keeps the MRU order and, where GLX or
`texture_from_pixmap` is missing, its snapshots of 1.0.0.
