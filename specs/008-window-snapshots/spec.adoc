== What and why

Each card and tile of the switcher shows a thumbnail of its window. The
focus watcher takes them: a snapshot of the active window every
`snapshot_interval` — 10 s by default, 1 s in the configurations of ws1 and
ws2 — and of a window 100 ms after it gets the focus. Every other window
shows the snapshot of its last focus, however old.

A snapshot costs 96–124 ms of a CPU on E1 for a window of 2560×1381
(research): the X server copies 14 MB into a shared memory segment made for
it, qws copies them again, swaps the channels pixel by pixel and scales them
down in Go. At 1 s the service spends some 9 % of a CPU all day doing so.

The author asked (2026-10-01) for snapshots that cost less, of every window,
not only the active one.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `0016519` (1.0.0). Unchanged:
thumbnails as images of at most 512×512 that both renderers draw, so the
frames of `cpu`, and of `glx` at rest, are drawn from them as before; the MRU
order and the tracking of the focus; the switcher.

== Decisions

Author, 2026-10-01:

* thumbnails scaled on the GPU, in qws: the pixmap of a window bound as a
  texture (`GLX_EXT_texture_from_pixmap`) — not scaled by RENDER in the X
  server, which measured as cheap but leaves no way to live thumbnails;
* every visible window, captured when its contents change (DAMAGE), each at
  most once a `snapshot_interval`;
* at an activation, the visible windows changed since their snapshot are
  captured again before the first frame;
* `snapshot_interval` defaults to 1 s;
* live thumbnails — windows changing on the cards while the switcher is
  shown — are a specification of their own, 009.

Made on the way, 2026-10-01, confirmed by the author: K1 asks for a p95
of 5 ms, not 2. The 2 came from a probe that captured every 50 ms, the GPU
awake; captured once a second, the GPU wakes from idle for each snapshot,
2.7 ms at the median, while the CPU waits on it and does nothing (research,
"Measured on E1").

== Behavior

. *Which windows.* Every client window of `_NET_CLIENT_LIST` that is
  viewable. A window the window manager unmaps — on a hidden workspace, a
  hidden tab — has no contents in the X server: it keeps the last snapshot
  taken while it was viewable, as now.
. *When.* A window is captured when it becomes viewable, 100 ms later to let
  it draw; when its contents change, at most once a `snapshot_interval`; and
  at an activation, before the first frame, if it changed since its
  snapshot — the activation waits for those at most 20 ms. Unchanged
  windows are not captured again.
. *How.* The pixmap of the window, named through Composite, is bound as a
  texture in a GL context of the snapshots' own, on a thread of their own; a
  shader averages it by area to at most 512×512; the thumbnail alone is read
  back. The pixmap stays bound while the window keeps its size and stays
  viewable.
. *While the switcher is shown* no snapshots are taken, as since
  specification 007, but for those of the activation.
. *Without* GLX, `GLX_EXT_texture_from_pixmap` or a compositor, the
  snapshots are those of 1.0.0: the active window, scaled on the CPU.
. `appearance.thumbnail.scaling_algorithm` applies to the snapshots on the
  CPU only; the GPU averages by area.
. `behavior.snapshot_interval` defaults to 1 s instead of 10 s — a changed
  default, a minor version.

== Metrics

Snapshots are logged at debug level, a record each: the window, its size,
the path (GPU or CPU), the cause and the time taken.

S1 — cost of a snapshot:: Wall time from binding the window to the thumbnail
in memory, per window.

S2 — idle CPU:: CPU time of the qws service over 10 minutes of the author's
desktop on E1 without an activation, from `/proc/<pid>/stat`.

S3 — freshness:: For a window changed and viewable, the time from its change
to its snapshot.

M1:: Activation latency, as in specification 001.

== Acceptance criteria

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| Cost: S1 p95 ≤ 5 ms on E1 for a window of 2560×1381 on the GPU path,
  against some 100 ms of 1.0.0.

| K2
| Idle CPU: S2 ≤ 0.5 % of a CPU on E1 with `snapshot_interval` 1 s, against
  some 9 % of 1.0.0.

| K3
| Coverage: every viewable client window gets a thumbnail; a changed window
  is captured within `snapshot_interval` + 0.2 s, one that becomes viewable
  within 0.5 s. Log, a session on E1.

| K4
| Quality: the thumbnail equals the area average of the window computed on
  the CPU within 1 per channel. Test in `pkg/glx` with an X display, GLX and a
  compositor, skipped without them, as the GLX test of specification 001.

| K5
| Activation: M1 p50 on `glx` not worse than 1.0.0 by more than 3 ms; S1 of
  specification 001.

| K6
| Fallback: without GLX or `texture_from_pixmap` the snapshots of 1.0.0 —
  test forcing the fallback.

| K7
| `go vet ./...` and `go test ./...` pass.
|===

The stalls of the X server on E1 (specification 007, research) are counted
for 10 minutes before and after, without a criterion: the snapshots of 1.0.0
are suspected.

== Results

2026-10-01, E1; the measurements in `research.adoc`.

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | How it is met

| K1 | S1 p50 2.66 ms, p95 3.92 ms for a terminal of 2556×1357 changing now
  and then; the first snapshot of a window, which names and binds its
  pixmap, 12–20 ms.
| K2 | 2.35 s of CPU in 601 s, 0.39 %, the active window changing every
  second; 1.0.0 9.0 %.
| K3 | Every viewable window captured within 15 ms of the start. In two runs
  of S1, whose warm-up shows every window in turn, all 31 windows of i3
  captured. `TestSchedule`: a settle after a change, at most once an
  interval.
| K4 | `TestGPUThumbnail`: within 1 of the area average on the CPU, exact for
  a pixmap no larger than a thumbnail, and the new contents after the
  pixmap is drawn anew and bound again; depths 24 and 32.
| K5 | S1, 20 activations of 3 steps, the branch then 1.0.0: M1 warm p50
  230.66 ms against 229.30 ms. The first pair, 1.0.0 first, gave 232.06
  against 180.53, but 1.0.0 went from 163–211 ms to 101–131 ms in its
  eighth activation, the time to list the windows — the same code — the
  machine, not the snapshots.
| K6 | `TestFallback`.
| K7 | `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...`.
|===

The X server, said to name a window's pixmap the events had shown
viewable, refused twice in a run of S1 while i3 switched workspaces; such a
window is captured again a settle later, up to three times, not on the
CPU.
