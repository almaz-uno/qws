== The pause

The two instances took 521 snapshots on change during the runs, none in a
second strictly between the first frame of an activation of the measured
instance and the end of its fade-out — some 1240 s of switcher shown in all.
The logs time their records to the second, so a snapshot in the second an
activation began or ended is not told apart.

The missed frames come in bursts: nine of the activations 10–20 of S4 miss
from 14 to 130 intervals each, the activations 16 and 17 of S3 with the
carousel 95 and 98 and four others of it 10 to 16, the rest at most six. Outside the bursts this change misses a fifth as many frames

measured instance no longer spinning while its switcher is shown, which 010's
build did. Its p95, though, is 7.92 ms against 7.09: more intervals run a
millisecond past the period without missing one. Neither the bursts nor the
shift are explained here; the other sessions on ws1 at the time are the
first suspects, and a run on an idle ws1 the way to tell.
