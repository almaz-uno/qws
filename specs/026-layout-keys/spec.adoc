== What and why

While the switcher is shown, `c` shows the carousel and `g` the grid, both
keys fixed in `pkg/ui/selector.go`; no key goes from one layout to the
other, and the overlay says nothing of them. In the grid, Left and Right
step through the tiles row after row, from the last to the first and back,
and Up and Down do nothing. The author asks (issue #78) for a key that
toggles the two layouts, a hint in the header saying what it does, and Up
and Down in the grid.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `fd00d27` — 1.3.4 with the
live thumbnails of 020 and 023 merged. Unchanged: `c`, `g`, Ctrl+C, the
main key, Left, Right, Enter and the cancel key; the layout every
activation opens in, `appearance.layout`; every frame without the hint —
the scenes of Σ without a header, and a header without the hint — byte for
byte; the band of the header and the geometry of both layouts; the
animations of 007 and 010; the live thumbnails of 020; the existing
configuration keys and their meaning.

== Decisions

=== Decided by the author, 2026-10-04

D1. *The key.* A configuration key `keybindings.layout_toggle`, `q` by
default, with the flag `--keybindings-layout-toggle` and the variable
`QWS_KEYBINDINGS_LAYOUT_TOGGLE`, as the other keybindings have. While the
switcher is shown it toggles the carousel and the grid. `c` and `g` stay as
they are. It follows their rule of modifiers: not with the workspace
modifier held. An empty value: no toggle, no hint. A name `keygrab` cannot
resolve: a warning, as the other keys report a bad name, no toggle, no hint.

D2. *Until the switcher closes.* The layout chosen with the key holds until
the switcher closes; the next activation opens in `appearance.layout`
again, as after `c` and `g` (`restoreInitialLayoutMode`).

D3. *The hint.* At the top right of the band of the header, what the key
does now: `Q — grid` in the carousel, `Q — carousel` in the grid — the
key's name as configured, a single letter upper-cased, any other name as
written, and an em dash. In the size and colour of the version of the
header, on its baseline, ending at the header's margin from the right edge.
None when the header is off (`appearance.header.enabled: false`) or the key
is empty or unresolvable. A field of `carousel.Config` the selector sets
from the layout and the key, drawn by `drawHeader`, so that `pkg/carousel`
knows nothing of keys.

D4. *Up and Down in the grid* move one row within the column, around it.
With n tiles in `cols` columns as the grid lays them out — row after row,
the last row from the left — Down from tile i goes to i + cols if it is
less than n, else to the top of the column, i mod cols; Up from i goes to
i − cols if it is not negative, else to the last tile of the column,
c + cols·⌊(n − 1 − c) / cols⌋ with c = i mod cols. A column of one tile:
no move. Left and Right unchanged. In the carousel Up and Down do nothing.
The move goes through the step of the arrows (`stepTo`), so the selection
frame slides; `cols` comes from the function the drawing uses.

=== Made in the specification, 2026-10-04, for the author to confirm

D5. *The layout key after the others.* The key is looked at after every
other key of the switcher: Enter, the cancel key, the workspace modifier,
`c`, `g`, Ctrl+C, the main key and the arrows. A name that is one of them —
`Tab`, `Escape`, `c` — keeps that key's meaning: there is no layout key,
no toggle and no hint, as for a name qws does not know, and one warning
says so as the selector starts; a hint of a key that toggles nothing would
say what it does not do (D3). Looked at before them, a layout key on the
main key would take the switching itself.

D6. *A narrow overlay.* The hint is not drawn where it would begin closer to
the end of the version — of the hostname when there is no version — than
the gap between the hostname and the version, 16 px. Rejected: cutting the
hint, which would show a name in part; moving it below the band, which
would change the band and the grid. With the font of the tests at the
size of E1, `Q — carousel` beside `ws1` and `v0.1.0` needs an overlay of
348 px (research): the rule is for overlays made narrow by a large font or
a large padding, not for the monitors of today.

D7. *What slides.* The selection frame slides to the tile above or below,
as it slides to the next tile with Right; around a column of three tiles or
more — from its last tile to its top, or back — it is there at once, as a
step around the list is at once (007, "A wrap-around step … is not
animated"). In a column of two tiles the tile around is the tile above or
below, and the frame slides. `stepTo` took any step of more than one window
for a wrap, which would put every row step at once (research); its callers
now tell it: Left and Right by the rule of before, Up and Down by D7.

D8. *The hint is a part of the header.* `drawHeader` draws it only when it
draws the hostname or the version, and the band of the header —
`headerBand`, below which the grid lays out its tiles — is computed without
it. So the grid has the same geometry with and without the hint, and
without a header no hint is drawn whatever the configuration says.

D9. *The scenes of Σ.* The six scenes with a header (005) get the hint of
the default key — the header as the selector now draws it — and their
digests change. Two new scenes keep the frame of E1 with the header of 005
alone, `carousel-e1-header-no-hint` and `grid-e1-header-no-hint`, with the
digests `carousel-e1-header` and `grid-e1-header` had; two more,
`carousel-header-narrow` and `grid-header-narrow`, 280 px wide, draw no hint
(D6).

== Behavior

=== The key

[cols="2,1,5"]
|===
| Key | Default | Meaning

| `keybindings.layout_toggle`
| `q`
| While the switcher is shown, toggles the carousel and the grid. A key name
  as for `keybindings.key` — `keysyms.yaml`, case-insensitive,
  `--keysym-list`. Empty: none.
|===

The flag `--keybindings-layout-toggle` and the variable
`QWS_KEYBINDINGS_LAYOUT_TOGGLE`, as in 002. An empty environment variable
counts as unset, as for every key (Viper): the empty value is given in the
file or with the flag. A name `keygrab` does not know is reported once, when
the selector is created, at warning level — "Failed to parse layout toggle
key, no layout toggle", with the name, as the cancel key reports its own —
and there is no toggle and no hint. Where the other keys fall back to a
default on a bad name, this one falls back to none (D1).

=== The toggle

. While the switcher is shown, the key pressed without the workspace
  modifier — alone, or with Shift or the primary modifier — shows the other
  layout: the grid from the carousel, the carousel from the grid. As `c`
  and `g` do: the layers of 007 are dropped and the frame drawn anew, at
  once; the selection, the hover and the workspace filter stay.
. The key is looked at after the other keys of the switcher (D5).
. The layout holds until the switcher closes — Enter, the release of the
  modifier, a click, the cancel key — and the next activation opens in
  `appearance.layout` (D2).

=== The hint

. *Text.* The key's name as configured, without the spaces around it — one
  letter upper-cased, any other name as written — an em dash between
  spaces, and the layout the key goes to: `Q — grid` in the carousel,
  `Q — carousel` in the grid, `F2 — grid`, `space — carousel`. A layout of
  `appearance.layout` other than `grid` is drawn as the carousel, and its
  hint is that of the carousel.
. *Place.* At the top right of the band of the header (005): in the face of
  the version, `appearance.font.size`, in the text colour at 0.6 of its
  alpha, on the baseline of the hostname and the version, ending 24 px — the
  margin of the header — from the right edge of the overlay. Drawn with the
  header, before the cards and the tiles.
. It changes with the layout — `c`, `g`, the key — and back to that of
  `appearance.layout` as the switcher closes.
. *None* with the header off, with the key empty or unknown, where it would
  come within 16 px of the version (D6), and in a frame without a header
  (D8).
. *Both renderers*: `drawHeader` draws it into the frame of `cpu`, and into
  the base layer of 007, `CarouselBase`, which the scenes of `glx` draw first
  in both layouts. A switch of layout drops the layers, so the base is drawn
  again with the hint of the new layout.

=== Up and Down

. In the grid, Down and Up move the selection a row within its column,
  around it, by the formula of D4, with the columns of `GridColumns` of
  `pkg/carousel`, the function `layoutGrid` lays the tiles out with. The
  selection frame slides to the tile above or below; around a column of
  three tiles or more it is there at once (D7). A column of one tile: no
  move, no frame.
. In the carousel Up and Down do nothing: no step, no frame.
. As Left and Right, with any modifier.

=== Existing configurations

No key, variable or flag is renamed or removed, and no default changes. A
configuration without the key gets `q` and its hint: a change the user
sees, with a new key — a minor version (constitution, "Releases and
versions"). `layout_toggle: ""` takes the toggle and the hint away; Up and
Down in the grid have no key. Before, `q` did nothing while the switcher was
shown. A configuration whose cancel key is `q`, as an example of
`doc/configuration.asciidoc` shows, keeps it: `q` cancels, with no hint and
a warning, and the toggle needs another name (D5). `README.md`, `doc/configuration.asciidoc` and
`config.yaml.example` describe the key, the hint and Up and Down in the same
change.
