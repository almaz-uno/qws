== Approach

*The font sets* (D1). `fontSets` of `pkg/ui/animation.go` becomes a free
list under a mutex: `get` takes the set given back last — its faces the
likeliest to be those needed, so that the sets used most carry most sizes
— or makes one when none is free; `put` gives it back. Nothing empties
it: there are as many sets as drawings have run at once — on ws1 the 12 of
`layerSlots` and the frame at rest — and each keeps the faces it made, once
per size, for the life of the process. The drawings on the main thread
keep the shared cache of `NewMultiFallbackFace`, as now.

*The glyph caches* (D2). `loadFontFaceFromPath` gives `truetype.NewFace`
`GlyphCacheEntries: 128`, a power of two as truetype requires. A glyph's
mask is rasterized into the entry its index and subpixel position hash to
and drawn before the next glyph is asked for; an entry is used again only
for the same glyph at the same position. The rasterizer's bounds are those
of the font's largest glyph, whatever the entries: a glyph rasterized again
has the bytes it had. Kept if K3 holds; else 256, and 512 with the finding
recorded.

*The canvases* (D3). A free list of pixel buffers in `pkg/carousel`
(`canvases.go`), under a mutex, ascending by capacity: a canvas of n bytes
takes the smallest free buffer of n to 1.5 n bytes, else a new one; a
buffer given back is kept while the list holds at most eight times the
largest it was given — eight frames. The canvases taken from it:

[cols="2,3,3"]
|===
| Canvas | Its pixels | Given back

| `newCanvas`: the frames of the carousel and of the grid, the bases
| A copy of the window background over every row, or `Clear` to its
  colour: every byte written
| A base once uploaded; a frame once the presenter no longer holds it
  (pkg/ui)

| `drawCard`: the band a card layer is drawn on
| Cleared, as `image.NewRGBA` makes it
| Once cropped, in `CardLayer`; at once when the card leaves nothing or
  is drawn again on the whole canvas

| `GridTiles`: the canvas of the tiles
| Cleared
| Once cropped

| `crop`: a card layer, the tiles layer
| Every row copied
| Once uploaded (pkg/ui)

| `CarouselHover`, `GridShadow`, `GridHover`, `GridSelection`
| Cleared
| Once uploaded (pkg/ui)

| `headCanvas`: the scratch canvas of the grid's heads, a `sync.Pool`
  now
| As they were: each head fills its rectangle before it draws there
  (019)
| When the tiles are drawn, every head waited for
|===

A canvas's bounds and stride are those of `image.NewRGBA`, on which alone
gg's rasterizer depends (019, research): the pixels are those of a canvas
made anew. A buffer is cleared a row at a time, as `copyRow` copies, so
that the collector need not wait for one long clear. Not changed:
`DrawPlaceholder`, drawn once a title; the pictures prepared for the frames
(`scratchImage`, a `sync.Pool` of 5 MB an activation, research); the
background kept by `newCanvas`.

*Who reads a canvas after it is drawn.* A layer: `SetLayer` copies it into
a texture with `glTexImage2D` from the process's memory, no pixel buffer
bound — done when the call returns — so `setLayer` gives it back, and a
result for layers dropped since, or a base already drawn on the spot, as
well. A frame: the GLX presenter keeps the last frame it presented
(`Present`, `PresentFaded` with a frame, `PresentStaged`) to upload only
the rows of the next one that differ, and the frame it stages in the pauses
(`StageFrame`) until it is presented; the PutImage presenter of `cpu` keeps
none. The selector keeps the same two, `held.shown` and `held.staged`, set
when a call succeeds, and a frame goes back once it is neither of them nor
the frame at rest drawn and not yet presented (`rest.ready`): the one shown
before a frame presented, the one staged before a frame staged, a frame at
rest dropped by a new target or a step cancelled, one drawn for a target
since left, the frame of `render` when its presentation failed — after its
dump. A failed call changes neither: the presenter then holds no more than
before. A new `Bind` holds none. Frames given to the presenter are only
those the selector drew, and the stills of the tests — `TestScenes`,
`TestLayers`, `TestParallelDrawing`, `TestGLXPresenterMatchesCPU` — are
never given back: their canvases come from the list but are not returned
to it.

*The memory given back* (D4). `hide`, as the overlay is unmapped, starts a
goroutine — one at a time — that drops the free canvases, for the
collector, and calls `debug.FreeOSMemory`: a collection and the idle heap
returned. The free canvases go with it: kept, they stay resident unused
until the next activation, and on a machine short of memory go to swap and
come back from it then; dropped, the next activation takes some 80 MB of
new pages, a fifth of what it allocated before (research, "The canvases: a
prototype").

*The measurements.* `BenchmarkE1Memory` for M1 and M2, `BenchmarkE1Grid`,
`BenchmarkE1Frame` and `BenchmarkE1Switch` for M3, from test binaries of
each commit run in turn, before and after each of D1, D2, and D3 with D4.

it is, `TestGLXPresenterMatchesCPU`, `TestLayers`, `TestParallelDrawing`;
the configuration; the frames, layers and records of the animations, of
the live thumbnails and of 028; the drawings on the main thread with the
shared cache of faces.
