== What and why

qws's heap swells to near a gigabyte at times and comes back to the system
only in part. The service of 1.5.0 on ws2, an hour after its start
(2026-10-05, 13:40, the profile of its pprof): 134 MB of heap in use, 84 MB
idle and kept, 958 MB of heap taken from the system at its peak; a resident
set of 326 MB, against 92 MB on ws1 at the same time. Of the heap in use,
some 50 MB are images of frames and layers (`image.NewRGBA`), some 42 MB the
glyph masks of font faces (`image.NewAlpha` under `truetype.NewFace`). In
the hour the drawing allocated 8.4 GB: 6.4 GB of images, 1.6 GB of glyph
masks.

On a machine short of memory the excess goes to swap — ws2 holds 6 to 12 GiB
under `mlock` for a test of its memory — and an activation waits for it to
come back (the delay at the release of Alt on ws2, 2026-10-05). The author
asked for the font faces to be bounded; the images are laid out with them.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `1de774d` (1.5.0). Unchanged:
every pixel of every frame and layer — the scene set Σ and its digests —
and the configuration.

== Where the memory goes

From the code (research):

* *A face is mostly its glyph cache.* `truetype.NewFace` allocates at once
  a mask buffer of `GlyphCacheEntries` glyphs, 512 by default, each the size
  of the font's widest glyph at that size: Noto Sans 1.11 MB at 20 px,
  6.55 MB at the 50 px of the header's hostname; DejaVu Sans 1.04 and
  6.25 MB — some 2.2 and 12.8 MB for the default chain of the two
  (research).
* *Faces per size, per set.* `carousel.Config.face` makes a face for each
  size it is asked — the font size scaled at each offset of the carousel,
  the header's two, those of the tiles and of the live labels — and caches
  it in the `FontSet` of the drawing, or in the shared cache of
  `NewMultiFallbackFace` for a drawing without one.
* *The sets are lost.* A drawing in parallel takes a `FontSet` from a
  `sync.Pool` (`fontSets`, `pkg/ui`): up to 12 at once on ws1, half of its
  24 CPUs (`layerSlots`), and one for the frame at rest. The collector
  empties a `sync.Pool` at every cycle or two; the next drawing makes its set
  and every face of it again — the 1.6 GB of masks in the hour. Found by 020
  (open decisions) for the frames at rest with live thumbnails on.
* *Images are made for each drawing.* A frame at rest of the overlay is
  2520×1400×4 bytes, 14.1 MB, on E1; a tiles layer up to that; a card layer
  some hundreds of kilobytes. Each drawing makes its canvas anew and drops it
  once uploaded — the 6.4 GB in the hour — and parallel drawings hold theirs
  at once: the peak of the heap.

== Decisions — recommended, for the author to confirm

D1. *The font sets kept.* A fixed free list of `FontSet`, under a mutex, in
place of the `sync.Pool`: as many as drawings run at once, never emptied by
the collector. A face is made once per size and set for the life of the
process. As 020 proposed.

D2. *Smaller glyph caches.* `GlyphCacheEntries` 128 instead of 512 — 32
glyphs at 4 subpixel positions, a quarter of the masks: 0.28 MB for Noto
Sans at 20 px, 1.64 MB at 50 px. A title draws some 20–40 distinct glyphs; a glyph out of the cache
is rasterized again. Kept only if the drawing is no slower (K3); else 256.

D3. *Image buffers reused.* The canvases of the frames at rest and of the
layers taken from free lists by size, returned once the presenter has
uploaded them (the GPU holds its copy), cleared by the drawing as now —
the new canvas starts as a copy of the background.

D4. *Memory given back after an activation.* Once the overlay is unmapped,
in the background, `debug.FreeOSMemory`: a cycle of the collector and the
idle heap returned to the system, off the path of every frame.

*Scope:* D1 and D2 are what the author asked — the faces. D3 and D4 take the
images and the peak with them. Recommended: all four, D1 and D2 first in
their own commits.

== Metrics

M1:: the heap in use, the heap taken from the system (`HeapSys`) at its
peak, and the resident set, after a scenario off the screen: E1's size,
33 windows with thumbnails of 512×288, 50 activations of steps through the
carousel and the grid, switches of the layout and live thumbnails, on the
presenter of the tests of `pkg/ui` — before and after.
M2:: the bytes allocated per activation in that scenario, by kind (images,
glyph masks).
M3:: the drawing times of 019 and 028 — `BenchmarkE1Grid`, the frames of the
carousel, `BenchmarkE1Switch` — before and after.
