== A face's glyph masks

`image.NewAlpha(maxw, maxh × GlyphCacheEntries)` at once, `maxw` and `maxh`
the font's bounds at the size plus 2, 512 entries unless set. Computed for
the default fonts at 72 dpi, a test outside the repository on ws1:

| DejaVu Sans, 128 entries | 0.13 MB | 0.26 MB | 0.34 MB | 1.56 MB

== The sets and the pool

`pkg/ui/animation.go`: `fontSets = sync.Pool{New: carousel.NewFontSet}`,
taken by the drawing of a frame at rest and by each layer drawn in the
background; `layerSlots` bounds those to half the CPUs — 12 on ws1. A
`sync.Pool` is emptied by the collector (its victim cache survives one
cycle): a set is lost within two cycles of not being used, 186 cycles in
the hour on ws2. 020, open decisions: the drawings of the frames at rest
found the pool empty a third more often with live thumbnails on.

== A set's faces

the titles of the carousel at 23 px times the scale of the cards at the
offsets 0 to 5, and its workspaces at 20 px times it; the grid's 18. Their

== The canvases: a prototype

D1–D4 on a copy of the code, 30 activations of the scenario a run,
2026-10-05 19:36 to 19:40; the free list of canvases two ways — kept
across activations, or dropped at the unmap before `debug.FreeOSMemory`:

[cols="3,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| | kept | dropped
| peak `HeapSys` | 318 | 342
| 5 s after: `HeapInuse`; `VmRSS` | 266; 342 | 181; 320
| kept: the heap; `VmRSS` | 251; 331 | 167; 309
| images an activation, 11–30 | 0 | 80
| glyph masks an activation, 1–10; 11–30 | 10.9; 0 | 13.0; 0
|===

Counted in the free list: at most 89 MB of canvases out at once, at most
84 MB free. Kept, those 84 MB stay resident while the overlay is unmapped
— on a machine short of memory they go to swap and come back from it at
the next activation; dropped, the next activation takes 80 MB of new
pages, a fifth of what it allocated before. The plan drops them.

A check of the frames given back, made once on that copy and undone: the
GLX presenter's last and staged frames compared, at each `Recycle`, with
the frame given back, and a panic if one of them. 12 activations of the
scenario and `BenchmarkE1Switch` passed; with the selector made to give
back the frame it had just presented, the scenario panicked at once.

The faces: the sets made 109–130 MB of glyph masks in the first ten
activations and none after; the heap kept grows by them, 82 to 167 MB.
