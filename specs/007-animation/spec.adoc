== What and why

The switcher changes its picture at once: a step replaces one frame of the
carousel with the next, the overlay appears and disappears in one frame, the
selection frame of the grid jumps to the next tile. The author wants these
changes animated and version 1.0.0 released after a good animation
(2026-10-01; constitution, "Releases and versions").

The CPU draws a frame of E1 in 33–40 ms (research), about 25 frames per
second with presenting — too slow for a smooth animation, which at the
144 Hz of E1 needs a new frame every 6.94 ms. Author's decision
(2026-10-01): on `glx` the GPU moves layers the CPU draws once; frames at
rest stay the frames of `cpu` bit for bit, frames in motion do not; `cpu`
keeps changing at once.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `73cfd5c`. Unchanged: the
picture at rest of both renderers — the frames `cpu` draws, and the scene
set Σ with its digests; the configuration; key and mouse handling apart from
what the animation adds.

== Decisions

Author, 2026-10-01: the approach above; the scope — the carousel step with
retargeting, the appearance and disappearance of the overlay, the selection
frame of the grid; 150 ms with ease-out, a new frame every vertical blank.

Made on the way, 2026-10-01, for the author to confirm — each for frames on
time (research, "Frames on time"):

* the background snapshots of the active window pause while the switcher is
  shown;
* the keyboard and modifier mappings are read once an activation, and again
  on `MappingNotify`, not on every key;
* K2 asks for no missed frame and a p95 half a millisecond above the
  period, not a p95 of one period: an interval measured at P varies by a
  tenth of a millisecond or more even when every frame makes its vertical
  blank, so half of them are above the period;
* the loop does not wait for the frame at rest: a step ends on time with
  its scene at the target, and the frame at rest follows when drawn — for
  the grid some 200 ms later;
* S2 ends each activation not with `Escape` but with steps back to the
  first window and the modifier released: an activation, for K6, that
  leaves the focus where it was.

== Behavior

All of it on `glx`; `cpu` behaves as today.

. *The carousel step.* The position of the selection, `p`, is continuous:
  a step sets a target, and `p` moves from where it is to the target in
  150 ms along ease-out cubic, `1 − (1 − u)³`. A key pressed mid-way sets a
  new target and restarts the 150 ms from the current `p`, so holding the key
  makes `p` follow it without queueing. A card at the fractional offset
  `o = index − p` is placed and scaled by the geometry of the carousel,
  continuous in `o`; its picture is the card as `cpu` draws it at the two
  integer offsets around `o`, drawn once into layers and cross-faded by the
  fractional part of `o`. The selection frame follows the card passing the
  centre. A wrap-around step — from the last window to the first or back —
  is not animated.
. *At rest* — when `p` reaches its target — the overlay shows the frame
  `cpu` draws for the target, byte for byte, as without animation, once it
  is drawn; until then, the scene at the target. The loop does not wait for
  it: a frame of the grid takes some 350 ms to draw on E1 (research).
. *The grid*: the selection frame and the shadow of the selected tile slide
  from its tile to the next in 150 ms, ease-out, under and over the tiles
  drawn without a selection; at rest, the frame of `cpu`.
. *Appearance*: the first frame of an activation fades in over 150 ms, from
  the end of its presentation; an `Expose` meanwhile is not answered. A
  quick press, released before the overlay shows, is not animated.
. *Disappearance*: on a selection — modifier released, `Enter`, a click —
  the chosen window is activated at once, and the overlay then fades out
  over 150 ms before it is unmapped; `Escape` fades it out too. The events
  read during a fade-out are handled after it; a key press, as of a new
  activation, cuts it short.
. *Pacing*: frames of an animation follow a timer at the refresh period of
  the monitor the overlay is on, from RandR; the swap interval stays 0 — an
  interval of 1 throttles the overlay to one swap a second (research). The
  layers go to the GPU in the pauses between frames.
. *While shown*, the switcher takes no snapshots of the active window, and
  reads the keyboard mapping once.

== Metrics

Frames of an animation are logged at debug level like the frames of
specification 001, with the kind of the animation — `carousel`, `grid`,
`fade-in`, `fade-out` — its progress and the time since the previous frame.
A frame of a step during a fade is a frame of both.

A1 — frame interval:: The time between two presented frames of one
animation, P to P.

A2 — step response:: From reading the `KeyPress` of a step to P of the first
frame of its animation.

A3 — duration:: From the first to the last frame of an animation.

Session S2:: As S1 of specification 001, and in each activation also a held
step key — 2 s at the author's autorepeat, 33 a second after 500 ms — before
`Escape`.
