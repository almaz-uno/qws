== Approach

The CPU draws layers, the GPU composes them. The layers of an activation:

* the *base* — the canvas with the background and the header, without
  cards (carousel) or without the selection (grid);
* a *card layer* per window and integer offset — the card as
  `drawWindowWithData` draws it at that offset, onto a transparent image of
  its bounding box; drawn on demand, in parallel, and kept for the
  activation;
* the layers of the *grid* — its tiles without a selection, with the hover,
  on a transparent image; the shadow of a selected tile under them and its
  selection frame over them, both moving.

The GLX presenter keeps them as textures and draws a frame as quads with
premultiplied blending, `BlendFunc(ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA)`, in the
z-order of the carousel. A card at a fractional offset is its two layers
of the integer offsets around it, placed and scaled to its geometry and
cross-faded; each is scaled by at most the ratio of the scales of
neighbouring offsets, so linear filtering does without mipmaps. At rest it
presents the frame `cpu` draws, as now; the CPU draws it while the
animation runs.

The layers are drawn in the background, each with fonts of its own — the
faces of `truetype` are not safe for concurrent use — on at most half the
CPUs, ahead of the step that needs them: the layers of the next step
either way are requested as soon as a step is set. The loop uploads them in
the pauses between frames and between events, a megabyte a pause at most.
The frame at rest, drawn in the background as well, goes to the GPU the
same way, into a second texture of the window size, and is presented by
swapping the two.

The fades scale every channel of what is presented — the scene, the frame
at rest, the last frame — by the alpha of the fade, in the same frames.

`cpu` keeps presenting the frames it draws, at once.

== Stages

Each stage leaves the program working and ends with K1 of 001, K10 and, from
stage 2 on, a run of S2 recorded in `research.adoc`.

. *Harness*: the records and S2, measured on `develop` code, where every
  "animation" is one frame.
. *The step*: layers, the scene of the presenter, the motion and the loop —
  K1, K2, K3, K4, K5, K8.
. *The grid*: the selection frame sliding.
. *The fades*: appearance and disappearance — K6.
. *The author's review* on E1 — K9; then the results, and 1.0.0.
