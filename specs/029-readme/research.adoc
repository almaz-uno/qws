== The pictures

`console/readme-demo` (`8ef1253`), run by `make readme-images`, draws
`doc/images/` at 2560×1440 and scales it down to 1280×720 by Catmull-Rom:
twelve windows drawn by the program — nvim showing `pkg/ui/motion.go` read
from the repository, a terminal with `make build` and `go test ./...`, a
browser, the PDF of 007, a chat with invented names, a player, a file
manager, a system monitor, a calendar, an image viewer, a mail client, `git
log --graph` — on a desktop with a wallpaper and i3bar; the header
`devbox v1.4.1`. The thumbnail size, the spacing and the font size are those
of the author's configuration (512, 600, 20): with the defaults (256, 300,
14) the cards come out at 128 px in the picture.

* The frames at rest are `Draw3DCarouselWithData` and `DrawGridLayout`.
* The frames in motion compose the layers of `pkg/carousel` as `pkg/ui`
  places them (`carouselItems`, `placeLayer`, `gridItems`) and as the
  presenter draws them: bilinear samples, colour `((1−w)·A + w·B)·alpha`,
  blend `ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`, 8 bits. A scene so composed at an
  integer position differs from the frame at rest of `cpu` by rounding
  only: 1.4 % of the channel values of the carousel, at most 4/255; 19 %
  of the grid's, at most 6/255 — the translucent tiles layer over the base,
  as on the GPU.
* Steps of 150 ms along ease-out cubic, a frame every 20 ms; at 144 Hz qws
  presents seven times as many.
* The animation is lossless WebP, 35 frames, 6.36 s, 1 965 842 bytes. The
  PNGs come out byte for byte the same on every run; the WebP did with
  libwebp 1.2.4 and may not with another version.
* No hover, no live thumbnails in the pictures: the windows do not change.
