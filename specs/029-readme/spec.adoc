== What and why

`README.md` lags behind qws. It ends with "Phase 4 is complete" and a list
of enhancements long made; it shows a tree of packages that no longer
exists; its section "Screenshots" has no picture; it names DejaVu Sans as
the font of the placeholders, while every text is drawn from the fonts of
`appearance.font.paths`, Noto Sans and DejaVu Sans by default. It says
nothing of what sets qws
apart: the animation at the refresh rate on the GPU, the frames at rest
byte for byte those of the CPU, the live thumbnails, the grid and its keys,
the measurements behind every one of them.

The author, 2026-10-05: a README that everyone reads and is struck by. The
constitution gives it its place — `README.md`: build, usage, features, an
overview of the configuration; the details in `doc/`.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `c3c598e` (1.4.1). Unchanged:
the code, the documents of `doc/`, the configuration.

== Decisions

D1 and D2 decided by the author on 2026-10-05, each the option recommended;
D3–D5 made in the specification.

D1. *The picture: drawn by qws itself.* At the top an animated picture — a
step of the carousel, a switch to the grid with `q`, a step of the grid —
drawn off the screen by qws's own drawing code from synthetic windows: a
terminal, an editor with qws's source, a browser, and the like, drawn by
the program that makes the picture. The frames in motion are composed of
the layers of `pkg/carousel` as the presenter of `glx` composes them (007):
the cards at their integer offsets cross-faded, the selection frame of the
grid moving over its tiles; the frames at rest are those of `cpu`. Nothing
of the author's screen. Rejected: a recording of the author's desktop,
which needs a desktop of windows fit to publish and the author at it; still
pictures only, which show nothing of the motion.

D2. *A landing page, then the engineering.* What qws is in a sentence and
the picture; the figures that set it apart; an install in a minute — the
release binary, its checksum, a `systemd --user` unit; the keys; the
features; a short section on how it keeps the refresh rate; the
configuration in brief, `doc/configuration.asciidoc` for the rest; building,
the specifications, releases, the licence. Rejected: a landing page alone,
the engineering in a document of its own; one reference of every key and
package.

D3. *Every figure from a measurement.* A figure the README gives comes from
the specification that measured it, on the machine it names, and links to
it; a claim of what qws does holds on `develop` (research: the table of the
claims and their sources). Nothing the README says is planned.

D4. *The pictures in the repository.* `doc/images/`: the animated picture
as WebP — GitHub shows animated WebP in a README — at most 3 MB; a still of
the carousel and of the grid as PNG, for where the animation does not play.
The program that makes them, `console/readme-demo`, runs with `make
readme-images`; it draws the same bytes on every run, the encoders' output
aside.

D5. *English,* as every document of the repository (017).

== Acceptance criteria

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| The claims: every statement of `README.md` about what qws does, and every
  figure, in the table of `research.adoc` with its source — a file of the
  code, a test, a specification and its criterion; none without one.

| K2
| The pictures: `make readme-images` makes `doc/images/` again; the PNG
  stills byte for byte as committed; the animation at most 3 MB, its frames
  in motion composed of the layers of `pkg/carousel`, its frames at rest
  those of `cpu`; no picture of the author's screen.

| K3
| The links: every relative link of `README.md` to a file of the
  repository resolves; every link to a specification names one that
  exists.

| K4
| `go build ./...`, `gofmt -l` empty, `go vet ./...`, `make check-specs` and
  `go test ./...` on every commit, in a worktree of its own.

| K5
| The author reads the README on GitHub, in the pull request: the picture
  plays, the page reads as the author wants it.
|===
