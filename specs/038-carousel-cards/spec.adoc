== What and why

The carousel puts the selected card in the middle at scale 1 and the others
beside it at `perspective + (1 − perspective)/(1 + 0.5·|k|)` — 0.87, 0.80,
0.76 with the default 0.6 —, `spacing` times the scale apart, not
overlapping. With the author's 512-px thumbnails and spacing of 600 on the
2520-px overlay of ws1 it shows five cards whole and two cut. The icon is
48 px times the scale, above the title, above the thumbnail.

The author, 2026-11-01: they look at the icons more than at the thumbnails;
make the carousel as the grid (037) — the side cards shrunk little, more
windows at once, the main icon as in the grid, the others shrunk little.
And a window that has no thumbnail yet gets one in the proportions of the
screen it is on, not a square.

The variants were tried on the author's desktop on ws1, a build of the
branch on Alt+F11 beside the service:

[cols="1,5,3"]
|===
| | Variant | The author

| A | cards overlapping; the icons on an arc, cardArc·k² lower, each card
  hanging from its icon | "выглядит плохо"
| B | A, the cards centred, the icon in the centre of the thumbnail | "норм"
| C | B, the icon 8 px above its card | —
| D | B, the icons on an arc above the box of the thumbnails, apart from
  them | —
| E | B, the icon as in the grid, its lower third on the thumbnail | "норм",
  taken
|===

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `3c8605a` (1.8.0). Unchanged:
the grid; the selection, hover and shadow of a card; the steps, their
animation and the live thumbnails, but for the order of the cards (D3).

== Decisions

D1, D2, D4 and D5 the author's, 2026-11-01; D3, D6 and D7 made in the
specification.

D1 (the author's). *Shrunk little.* The centre card at scale 1, its
neighbours at 0.85, each card further 0.02 less: 0.83, 0.81, 0.79, 0.77
at the fifth. Before: 0.87, 0.80, 0.76, 0.73, 0.71.

D2 (the author's). *Overlapping.* The neighbours of the centre card are
24 px from it; each card further lies under the one before it, nearer the
centre, and shows 0.27 of the nominal width — `appearance.thumbnail.width`
— beyond it. A side card is placed by its outer edge, so that a narrow
window shows that slice too. On ws1 (512 px, 2520 px): 138 px a card,
eleven cards in view, the fifth cut by 8 px; before, five whole and two
cut.

D3. *The nearest on top.* The cards are drawn from the furthest from the
centre to the centre one, on the CPU and in the scenes of the presenter;
the mouse picks the topmost card under it; the holes of a live thumbnail
at rest are where its own icon and the cards drawn after it lie.

D4 (the author's). *The icons as in the grid.* The icon of a card is
72 px times the square root of its scale — 72 in the middle, 66 to 63
beside it —, centred on what is seen of the card, its lower third on the
thumbnail; drawn after the thumbnail and the frames. The title and the
workspace go under the thumbnail, centred on what is seen of the card and
cut to its width.

D5 (the author's). *The placeholder in the proportions of the monitor.* A
window without a thumbnail gets a placeholder 512 px wide, as high as the
monitor it lies most on makes it — the monitor under the pointer when its
place is unknown, the overlay without an X connection —, kept by window,
drawn anew when its title changes. Before: a square of 256, kept by title.

D6. *The cards centred.* Every card is centred on the middle of the
overlay, no arc: the icons of windows of different proportions stand at
different heights.

D7. *Two keys no longer used.* `appearance.spacing` and
`appearance.perspective`, their flags and variables are read as before —
files that set them stay valid — but do not change the carousel; the
documentation says so. `Draw3DCarousel`, the carousel of thumbnails alone
no part of qws calls, keeps them as it is.
