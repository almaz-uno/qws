== What and why

qws runs on several machines of the author, and the overlay does not say
which machine it belongs to, nor which build of qws draws it. The author
asked for the version at the top left of the overlay and, next to it, the
current hostname in large letters (2026-09-30).

The specification is a delta to `develop` with specification 001 merged
(`e094c55`). It changes the picture of both renderers, which specification
001 made one picture. Unchanged: everything else in the picture, the layouts
and their input, the configuration.

== Behavior

. *The header*: in the top-left corner of the overlay, 24 px from its left
  and top edges, the hostname (`os.Hostname`, as it is) in the text colour
  at 0.9 of its alpha, at 2.5 times `appearance.font.size`; after it, with a
  gap of 16 px and on the same baseline, the version — what `qws version`
  prints — in the text colour at 0.6, at `appearance.font.size`. Without a
  hostname only the version is drawn.
. *Both layouts* draw it, right after the background, before the cards. In
  the grid, the tiles are laid out below the band of the header — its top
  margin, the line of the hostname and a gap of 16 px — instead of over the
  whole window; the carousel keeps its geometry, its cards are well below
  the band.
. *No configuration*: the header is always drawn. Without a hostname and a
  version — which only a test can arrange — nothing is drawn, and the
  picture is that of specification 001.

Interpretation, open to the author's correction: "large letters" is taken
as a large font, not as capitals; the hostname is shown as the system gives
it.
