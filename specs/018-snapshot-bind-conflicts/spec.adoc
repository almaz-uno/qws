== What and why

Specification 008 binds the pixmap of each viewable window as a texture
(`GLX_EXT_texture_from_pixmap`) and keeps it bound while the window keeps
its pixmap. A second instance of qws beside the service — a test instance, a
measurement run, a second service — gets every binding refused on ws1: X
error 11, BadAlloc, on `glXCreatePixmap` (request 152.22, GLX being
extension 152 there), then 161, GLXBadPixmap, on `glXBindTexImageEXT`
(152.16). Every snapshot of that instance then falls back to the CPU path of
1.0.0, 97–111 ms each, the cost 008 removed; an idle second instance spends
some 10 % of a CPU. Its log said "X error 161", the second error, a
consequence of the first, without the request.

The cause, confirmed by a probe (research): the X server of ws1, NVIDIA's
GLX, lets one GLX pixmap exist on a pixmap's storage at a time — a second
`glXCreatePixmap` on it, from the same client or another, with the same
FBConfig or another, through the same pixmap or another name Composite gave
the window, gets BadAlloc until the first is destroyed. GLX documents it:
"BadAlloc is generated if there is already a GLXFBConfig associated with
pixmap". The service holds the GLX pixmap of every viewable client window;
picom holds those of the frames of i3, the top-level windows, which are
other pixmaps, so it does not conflict with the service.

The change: between the GPU and the CPU, the window's pixmap is scaled in
the X server by RENDER — measured in 008 at 0.55–0.93 ms (research of 008,
"Scaling in the X server") — and the error of a refused binding names its
request.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `f90abc7` (1.2.0). Unchanged:
which windows are captured and when (008); a window whose pixmap the GPU
binds, captured as before; thumbnails of at most 512×512; without GLX or
`texture_from_pixmap` the snapshots of 1.0.0; the configuration — no key,
variable or flag changes.

== Decisions

Asked for, 2026-10-01:

* a RENDER path when the GPU will not bind a window — BadAlloc,
  GLXBadPixmap, or no FBConfig of its depth: the pixmap halved by bilinear
  filtering, a 2×2 box at exactly ½, while more than twice the thumbnail,
  then one bilinear pass, `RepeatPad` on the pictures, into a pixmap of the
  thumbnail's size, read back with MIT-SHM `ShmGetImage` into a segment made
  once and reused;
* the CPU path only when RENDER fails too;
* the request in the error of `BindPixmap`: "X error 11 on request 152.22".

Made on the way, 2026-10-01, for the author to confirm:

* *The GPU is tried again when the pixmap is named anew, not at each
  capture.* A window the GPU would not bind is taken by RENDER until its
  pixmap is named anew — it becomes viewable again or changes size, as at
  every return to its workspace under i3 — and the GPU is then tried first
  again. The client holding the GLX pixmap holds it, as qws and picom do,
  for as long as the window keeps that pixmap, so a retry before then fails
  unless that client has ended; and each failed attempt costs two round
  trips and its X errors. What it costs not to retry: an instance that lost
  a window to another — a service restarted beside a test instance — keeps
  it on RENDER after the other ends, until the window is named anew. RENDER
  takes no more wall time than the GPU — S1 p50 1.64 ms (K2) against
  2.66 ms of 008 — but its work is done by the X server.
* *Any refused binding goes to RENDER*, whatever its error, as does a depth
  no FBConfig binds.
* *Each side scaled on its own.* A side is halved while more than twice its
  side of the thumbnail, and the last pass scales from the window's own
  extent at that scale, not from the halved pixmap, which an odd side pads
  by a pixel; the prototype of 008 scaled both sides by one factor taken
  from the width. On the windows of ws1, 0.47–1.57 per channel from the area
  average against the prototype's 0.94–3.06 (research).
* *The first X error of the binding is told*, with its request; those after
  it in the same call are its consequences.

== Behavior

. *RENDER.* A window whose pixmap `BindPixmap` refuses keeps the pixmap
  named, and its snapshots are scaled from it in the X server by RENDER:
  each side halved — a bilinear sample at the corner of four pixels — while
  more than twice the thumbnail's, then one bilinear pass to the thumbnail's
  size, the pictures padded at their edges. The thumbnail, of the size of
  008, is read back over a shared memory segment of 1 MB made when the
  snapshotter starts; its alpha is ignored, as on the GPU and the CPU.
. *Retried on the GPU* when the pixmap is named anew — a map, an unmap, a
  change of size (008, "Found on the way") — and not before.
. *The CPU*, as in 008, when RENDER fails for a window, and for every such
  window without RENDER, MIT-SHM or the format `a8r8g8b8`, or with an X
  server whose images are not LSBFirst.
. `appearance.thumbnail.scaling_algorithm` keeps applying to the CPU path
  only; RENDER filters as above.
. *Log*, at debug level: a refused binding once per naming, "Pixmap not
  bound on the GPU until named anew", with the error; the record "Snapshot"
  of 008 with the path `render`; "Snapshot failed, taken on the CPU", with
  the path that failed, instead of "Snapshot on the GPU failed, taken on the
  CPU". Without RENDER, at info level, once: "RENDER unavailable, windows
  the GPU will not bind are captured on the CPU".
. *The error of `glx.BindPixmap`*: "glx: binding pixmap 0x…: X error 11 on
  request 152.22" — the first X error of the call, with the major and minor
  code of its request.

== Metrics

S1 — cost of a snapshot:: As in 008: `ms` of the record "Snapshot", per
path. The first snapshot of a window, which names its pixmap and tries the
GPU, is counted apart.

Run R:: The build as a second instance on E1 beside the service of 1.2.0
and picom, idle, for 30 s: `QWS_LOG_FORMAT=json qws -v -m Alt -k F12`
stopped by SIGINT (`timeout -s INT 30`), its log at debug level, its CPU
time from `times` of the shell that ran it. R-before runs the build of
`f90abc7`, R-after that of this change.

== Acceptance criteria

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| Error: the refused bindings of R-after are logged as "X error 11 on
  request 152.22".

| K2
| Path and cost: in R-after every snapshot of a window the GPU refused is
  taken by RENDER, none on the CPU; S1 by RENDER p95 ≤ 10 ms, a tenth of the
  CPU path's, for windows of 2556×1357 and 2556×1392.

| K3
| Quality: `TestRenderThumbnail` — a pixmap scaled by RENDER is within a
  mean of 1.2 per channel of the area average computed on the CPU, on an
  image about as hard to scale as the windows of E1, and comes out as it is
  when no larger than a thumbnail; depths 24 and 32. Skipped without an X
  display with RENDER and MIT-SHM.

| K4
| No retry within a pixmap's lifetime: in R-after, one record "Pixmap not
  bound" per window and naming of its pixmap, however many snapshots
  follow.

| K5
| Unchanged: `TestGPUThumbnail`, `TestFallback`, `TestSchedule`,
  `TestNewAndClose` pass.

| K6
| `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...` and `make check-specs` pass.

== Open decisions

. A retry on the GPU within a pixmap's lifetime — say once a minute —
  should an instance be seen keeping windows on RENDER after the client that
  held them has ended.
. Mesa: whether its GLX refuses a second GLX pixmap of a storage as NVIDIA's
  does, and so whether the RENDER path is ever taken on ws3, is not known;
  ws2 and ws3 were not tried.
