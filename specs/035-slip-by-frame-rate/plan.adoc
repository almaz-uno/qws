== Approach

*The window* (D2, D3). `autoAnimation` keeps, in place of the ring of 60
lateness flags and its count, a ring of the intervals of the frames
counted, `slipFrames` (512) long, with their number and their sum.
`frame(interval, period)` adds the interval, drops the oldest while the
others still span `slipSpan` (0.5 s) — and the oldest when the ring is
full —, and makes it still when the window spans `slipSpan` or more and
`frames × period < slipShare × span`, `slipShare` 0.75: the mean rate
below 75 % of the refresh rate, in integers of nanoseconds but for the
share. The period is the one passed with the frame, as now. `begin`
starts the window anew at the probe, as it started the ring. Which frames
are counted, and once at the longest interval of their records, stays in
`Selector.frame` (`pkg/ui/animation.go`) as it is; its comment on the
third late is changed.

*The record* (D5). `become` logs "Animation still: the frames slip" with
`fps` — frames over span —, `refresh_hz`, `frames` and `window_ms`; the
period it needs is kept by `frame` when it makes it still.

*The tests.* `TestSlipCount` builds windows at the periods of 60, 144 and
165 Hz: a pause of 100 ms and of 150 ms among on-time frames, a pause on
either side of the edge of 75 % — the window exactly 0.5 s of m on-time
frames and the pause, (m + 1)·period against 0.75 × 0.5 s —, a load of
30 ms from an empty window, a window not yet full, 512 frames in less
than 0.5 s, the window across activations, the probe, the kinds, off.
`TestSlipReplay` replays A, B, `fast-ws1.txt` as recorded and after a
window of on-time frames, B scaled to 60 Hz, and B followed by a load,
reporting the lowest share of a full window. `TestStillFrames` fills the
window with on-time frames of 7 ms before each step it trips at a period
of 1 ns: the first frame counted makes it still.
