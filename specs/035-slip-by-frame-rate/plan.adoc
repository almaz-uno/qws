== Approach

*No detector* (D1). `autoAnimation` keeps the look for a viewer, `begin`
and the records of the viewer; the ring of the frames, `frame`, the probe
and its clock, `countsSlips` and the record of the frames go. `frame` of
`pkg/ui/animation.go` no longer counts; `logAnimationFrame` no longer
returns the interval it logs.

*The state* (D2). The selector keeps the setting, `animMode`, what the key
left, `forced` — none, on, off —, and what the activation is, `animState`:
on — the animations of the configuration —; off — none, the live
thumbnails as when animated, `enabled: false` —; still — none, no live
thumbnails. `setAnimation` sets it with `anim`, `still`, the durations of
the step and the fade and the hint, as `setStill` did. Under `false` the
animations are read as under `true`, for the key to turn them on;
`parseAnimation` stays as it is. `beginAnimation` sets the state the key
and the setting give before the first frame — under auto that of the last
activation — and looks for a viewer only when the key does not hold;
`decideAnimation` decides it as the first frame is drawn and reports
whether the hint changed, and `render` then draws the frame again before
presenting it (D6).

*The key* (D2, D4). `keybindings.animation_toggle` in `config.Keybindings`,
`Default()`, `setDefaults`, the flag of `cmd/qws` — as `layout_toggle`. The
name resolved by `parseToggle`, shared with `parseLayoutToggle`, each with
its warnings; the keys taken those of the layout key and the layout key.
`handleKeyPressSimple` takes the press after every other key, without the
workspace modifier, on a presenter that composes: the key down. The loop
takes the release: if the key was pressed in the activation and the server
has it up — `QueryKeymap`, a hook for the tests —, `toggleAnimation`: on to
still by `stillNow`, off or still to on; the key holds; the layers dropped
and the frame drawn anew with the new hint; the live thumbnails asked for
when on. `beginAnimation` forgets a press of the last activation.

*The hint* (D3, D5). `carousel.Config.AnimationHint`, set by `setAnimation`
from the key's name and the state, none without the header, the key or a
presenter that composes. `drawHints` of `pkg/carousel` draws the layout
key's and then the animation key's in a row from the right, `hintGap`
32 px apart, stopping at the first that does not fit; the band as it is.
