== What and why

The author, 2026-10-13: on a screen of 144 Hz and more, stepping fast
through the carousel turns the animation off — `enabled: auto` goes still,
"Animation still: the frames slip" —; on a screen of 60 Hz it does not.
ws2's two screens of 2560×1440 run at 164.83 Hz, a period of 6.07 ms; its
journal of 2026-10-13: still at 13:54, back at the probe at 14:05:34 and
still again 19 s later, 3 late of 11 frames counted. On ws1 at 144 Hz on
2026-10-14 the count went still twice, both falsely (research, "Measured
and dropped").

The rule of 031 (D5) counts a frame late when its interval is above 1.25
refresh periods: at 165 Hz a slack of 1.5 ms, within the jitter of the
frame timer; the frames recorded by 031 keep 0.99 of the refresh rate in
the mean, a viewer connected or not. A rule by the mean rate over 0.5 s
was measured and would have kept every recorded frame animated (research);
the author chose the simpler design instead: no detector of the frames at
all, and a key the user turns the animation on and off with.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `f1cfb59` (1.6.1 with 036)
and to 031. Unchanged, of 031: the setting `appearance.animation.enabled`
and its values, `true` and `false` as they are; `vnc_ports`; the viewer of
D4 and what still is (D3); the records of the viewer (D7). Removed, of 031:
the frames that slip (D5, its O1), the probe (D6), the record "Animation
still: the frames slip". Unchanged, of 026: the layout key, its hint, its
rule of modifiers.

== Decisions

D1–D3 decided by the author on 2026-10-14; D4–D8 made in the
specification.

D1 (the author's). *Auto by the viewer alone.* `enabled: auto` is still
only while a VNC viewer is connected on a port of `vnc_ports` (D4 of 031).
The frames are not counted: nothing goes still by the frames, nothing is
probed. `true` and `false` as now.

D2 (the author's). *The key.* `keybindings.animation_toggle`, `a` by
default, with the flag `--keybindings-animation-toggle` and the variable
`QWS_KEYBINDINGS_ANIMATION_TOGGLE`, as the layout key of 026 has them; its
name resolved as that key's, the same rule of modifiers — not with the
workspace modifier —; an empty name no toggle and no hint; a name `keygrab`
cannot resolve, or one of another key of the switcher — Enter, the cancel
and main keys, `c`, `g`, the arrows, the layout key — a warning, no toggle,
no hint. Pressed and released while the switcher is shown, it turns the
animation over from what it is now — animated to still, still or off to
animated — over whatever the setting says: auto with a viewer, on; auto
without one, off; `true` off, `false` on. At once in the activation:
turned off, what moves is at its target and the live thumbnails stop, as
still is (D3 of 031); turned on, the next steps move and the live
thumbnails start. It holds for the next activations until qws restarts or
the key is pressed again — the author's words "можно включить
принудительно … можно выключить принудительно"; that it holds across
activations, not to the end of one, is the specification's choice, and
nothing is written to disk.

D3 (the author's). *The indicator.* In the header, beside the hint of the
layout key (026, D3): `A — animation on` or `A — animation off`, what the
activation is now — the key's name as configured, one letter upper-cased,
any other as written —; the face, the colour and the baseline of the
version, as the hint of 026. None with the header off, with the key empty
or unresolvable (D3 of 026).

D4. *On release.* The key acts as it is released — "нажав (и отпустив)" —
not as it is pressed. A key held repeats on X as a release and a press,
the release with the key still down: as a release comes, qws asks the
server whether the key is down (`QueryKeymap`, a round trip, once a
release of the key) and ignores it if it is. The press is taken only while
the switcher is shown: a key pressed in one activation and released in the
next does nothing.

D5. *The two hints in a row.* The hints are laid out from the right edge:
the layout key's ending at the margin of the header, as now; the animation
key's left of it, ending 32 px — twice the gap of the header — before it,
or at the margin without the layout key's hint. A hint that would come
closer than the gap of the header to the version (026, D6) is not drawn,
nor any left of it: an overlay too narrow for both draws the layout key's
alone. The band of the header and the grid are as without the hints.
Rejected: the animation hint right of the layout key's, which would move
the hint of 026 with the state.

D6. *The first frame.* The first frame of an activation is drawn while the
viewer is looked for (D4 of 031) and before it is known: it shows the hint
of the state the last activation was decided to, the key's if it holds.
When the look says otherwise — a viewer come or gone since —, the frame is
drawn again with the right hint before it is presented: one frame more in
the activation after a viewer comes or goes, none otherwise. With the key
holding, no viewer is looked for.

D7. *The records.* At info level, once a change: "Animation still: a VNC
viewer connected", "Animation back" as in 031 — not while the key holds,
since no viewer is looked for then —, and "Animation toggled on by the
key", "Animation toggled off by the key".

D8. *Under cpu.* Nothing moves under `cpu` whatever the keys say (031):
no toggle and no hint.

== Behavior

=== The key

[cols="2,1,5"]
|===
| Key | Default | Meaning

| `keybindings.animation_toggle`
| `a`
| While the switcher is shown, pressed and released, turns the animation
  on and off over `appearance.animation.enabled`, until qws restarts or it
  is pressed again. A key name as for `keybindings.key`. Empty: none.
|===

=== Existing configurations

No key, variable or flag is renamed or removed, and no default changes. A
configuration without the key gets `a` and its hint, and `a` that did
nothing while the switcher was shown turns the animation over: a change
the user sees, with a new key — a minor version (constitution, "Releases
and versions"). `animation_toggle: ""` takes the toggle and the hint away.
A configuration whose layout or cancel key is `a` keeps it, with a warning
and no animation key. `enabled: auto` no longer goes still by the frames:
a configuration that relied on it — a VNC viewer on a port not listed in
`vnc_ports` — is animated, and the key turns it off.

=== The scenes

The scenes of Σ keep their digests: the hint of the animation key is a
field of `carousel.Config` of its own, `AnimationHint`, empty in them, and
their frames are those with `animation_toggle: ""`. Six scenes are new:
`carousel-e1-header-animation-on`, `-off`, `grid-e1-header-animation-on`,
`-off` — the frame of E1 with both hints —, and
`carousel-header-narrow-animation`, `grid-header-narrow-animation` — 280 px,
no hint, with the digests of the `-header-narrow` scenes.
