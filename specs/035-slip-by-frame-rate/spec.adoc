== What and why

The author, 2026-10-13: on a screen of 144 Hz and more, stepping fast
through the carousel turns the animation off — `enabled: auto` goes still,
"Animation still: the frames slip" —; on a screen of 60 Hz it does not.
ws2's two screens of 2560×1440 run at 164.83 Hz, a period of 6.07 ms; its
journal of 2026-10-13: still at 13:54, back at the probe at 14:05:34 and
still again 19 s later, 3 late of 11 frames counted.

The rule of 031 (D5) counts a frame late when its interval is above 1.25
refresh periods: 7.58 ms at 165 Hz, a slack of 1.5 ms, against 4.2 ms at
60 Hz. The frames recorded by 031 on ws2 at 165 Hz
(`pkg/ui/testdata/vnc-a.txt`, `vnc-b.txt`) say what those late frames
are: their intervals come in pairs — 8.3 ms, then 3.6 ms — the schedule
of the frames (`frameDue`) catching up; none is above 2 periods, none
misses its slot, even with a viewer connected; the mean interval is 0.99
of a period in either batch, 160 fps in the worst window of 30 frames with
a viewer, 164 without. The rule counts the jitter of the frame timer, not
frames lost: at 165 Hz the slack is within the jitter, which grows when
fast steps draw their layers beside the frames.

ws1, 2026-10-14, 144 Hz (research): the count went still twice, both
falsely — at 3 late of 35 frames counted, two of them from a step seven
hours before the third; and after the probe at 3 late of 11, in a fast
step whose long intervals, after layers of the cards uploaded, are each
followed by frames at once: the 11 at 141 fps of 144.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `d6d535a` (1.6.1) and to 031.
Unchanged, of 031: the setting and its key; the viewer of D4; the probe
of D6; the records of D7, but the fields of one (D5 here); which frames
are counted (its D5 as amended, its O1); the pictures.

== Decisions

D1 decided by the author on 2026-10-13, with the numbers of D2 and D3 as
a draft; D2–D5 made in the specification, the numbers settled by the
research on 2026-10-14 (O1).

D1 (the author's). *By the frame rate.* The frames slip when their mean
rate falls, not when some intervals are long: the mean of the intervals of
the frames counted, over a window, against the refresh period. A frame
late is followed by one early while the schedule catches up, and the two
cancel in the mean; a slot lost does not.

D2. *A window of time.* The frames counted of the last 0.5 s of
animation — the latest frames counted whose intervals sum to 0.5 s or
more, the fewest such —, not the last 60 frames: 60 frames are 1 s at
60 Hz and 0.36 s at 165 Hz; a window of time is the same on every screen.
The window runs on across the activations, as the count of 031, and the
probe of D6 starts it anew. The first frame of an animation has no
interval and is not counted, as now: the pauses between the animations
are not in the mean. The window holds 512 frames at most: as many within
0.5 s are above 1000 fps; holding them in less, it is not full.

D3. *A share of the refresh rate, one window.* Still when the window is
full — its frames span 0.5 s — and its mean rate — its frames over the
sum of their intervals — is below 75 % of the refresh rate: 124 fps at
165 Hz, 108 fps at 144 Hz, 45 fps at 60 Hz; at 75 % not. A load goes
still once the window is full, in 0.51–0.55 s of animation from an empty
window, 0.15–0.3 s from one full of on-time frames. A single pause among on-time
frames goes still above some 132 ms at 144 and 165 Hz, 142 ms at 60 Hz:
one of 100 ms keeps the window at 81–84 %, one of 150 ms takes it to
71–74 % and makes it still (research, "The choice"). Rejected: 1 s,
which keeps pauses up to some 257 ms but takes 1 s of animation to fill;
66 %, which keeps a display losing every third slot animated; two windows
in a row, which keep any single pause but take up to 1.1 s on a load.

D4. *VNC by its viewer.* At 165 Hz the frames of a viewer connected keep
their rate — batch A of 031 98.7 % of it, 162.7 fps, in its worst window
(research) —; the mean does not see a viewer, and D4 of 031 does — on the
ports of `vnc_ports`. K2 of 031 — batch A goes still within its first
three activations — no longer holds. A viewer on a port not listed goes
still by the frames only when it costs frames: the second part of K7 of
031, not tried there, is given up.

D5. *The record.* "Animation still: the frames slip" carries the mean rate
of the window, `fps`, the refresh rate, `refresh_hz`, the frames of the
window, `frames`, and its span, `window_ms`, instead of the late frames
and the frames counted of 031; the other records of D7 of 031 as they
are.

== Outside this change

The layers of the cards uploaded in the pause before a frame, 1.2–1.3 MB,
cost it 10–12 ms of presenting on ws1 at 144 Hz, two slots lost (research,
"Finding"): a real stall, which the mean of D1 tolerates, left for a later
specification.

== Open decisions

O1. *The window and the share of D2 and D3.* Settled by the research on
2026-10-14: 0.5 s and 75 %, one window, as the draft said; a single pause
of 150 ms makes it still, one of 100 ms does not (research, "The
choice"). Open for the author until the trial (K5).
