== What and why

A tile of the grid draws the icon of its window at 24 px, in its top-left
corner, 8 px from the edges, over the thumbnail (`gridIconSize`,
`gridIconPadding`). On the tiles of ws1 — thumbnail boxes of some
380×210 at 47 windows (036) — such an icon is hard to make out.

The author, 2026-10-27: make the icons larger, some three times, at the top
centre or in the centre of a tile — try and compare. The variants were
compared first on the stills of the README demo (2560×1440, thumbnails of
512), then on the author's desktop on ws2, each a build of the branch on
Alt+F11 or Alt+F8 beside the service:

[cols="1,5,3"]
|===
| | Variant | The author

| A | 24 px in the corner, as now; 72 px in the corner, at the top centre, in
  the centre; 48 px at the top centre — stills only | 72 px at the top
  centre
| B | 72 px at the top centre, 8 px below the top of the thumbnail box, over
  the thumbnail | the icon looks a part of the thumbnail
| C | 16 px above the top edge of the tile — 20 px of spacing between the
  rows leave no more | the frames of the tile, of the selection and of
  the hover cross the icon
| V1 | a band of 80 px above the thumbnail box, the icon in it, 8 px above
  the box | "not bad", kept as V1
| V2a | a band of 48 px, two thirds of the icon: its lower third lies on
  the box, the thumbnail centred in it as now | over a wide window the
  icon barely reaches the thumbnail
| V2b | V2a, the thumbnail at the top of its box | taken
|===

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `30dd885` (1.7.0). Unchanged:
the carousel, but for the icon it is given (D4); the width of tiles and
thumbnail boxes, the columns, the least thumbnail and the rule of the
scroll (036), the titles, frames, selection and live thumbnails; the
configuration.

== Decisions

D1–D3 the author's, 2026-10-27, after the comparison; D4 and D5 made in the
specification.

D1 (the author's). *72 px.* The icon is drawn in a square of 72 px, three
times the 24 of now, in its proportions.

D2 (the author's). *At the top centre, its lower third on the thumbnail.*
A tile gets a band of 48 px — two thirds of the icon — between its top
padding and its thumbnail box. The icon is centred across the tile at the
top of the band, so that its lower 24 px lie on the thumbnail. The
thumbnail is centred across its box and put at the top of it, not in the
middle as now: the icon lies on every thumbnail alike whatever the
proportions of its window, and the room a wide window leaves is below it,
above the title. The tile is 48 px taller where its height is bounded —
up to `appearance.thumbnail.height` + 108 instead of + 60, and a box in
the proportions of the overlay in a grid that scrolls; where the tiles
share the height of one screen, the box is 48 px lower.

D3 (the author's). *No key.* The size and the place are fixed, as they are
now; the configuration keys of the trial are dropped.

D4. *An icon not upscaled.* `_NET_WM_ICON` is read at the size closest to
128 px, larger rather than smaller — 48 px now —, so that an application
giving several sizes is drawn at 72 by downscaling. The icons by class
already prefer `hicolor/128x128`. The carousel draws the same icon at
48 px times the scale of its card, now from the larger image. The cost: 64
KiB of pixels a window read and kept instead of 9 KiB.

D5. *No wider than the box.* On a tile whose thumbnail box is less than
72 px wide the icon is as wide as the box.
