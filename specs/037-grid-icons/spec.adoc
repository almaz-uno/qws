== What and why

A tile of the grid draws the icon of its window at 24 px, in its top-left
corner, 8 px from the edges, over the thumbnail (`gridIconSize`,
`gridIconPadding`). On the tiles of ws1 — thumbnail boxes of some
380×210 at 47 windows (036) — such an icon is hard to make out.

The author, 2026-10-27: make the icons larger, some three times, at the top
centre or in the centre of a tile — try and compare. Compared on the stills
of the README demo (2560×1440, thumbnails of 512): the icon of 24 px in the
corner as now; of 72 px in the corner, at the top centre, in the centre; of
48 px at the top centre. The author chose 72 px at the top centre.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `30dd885` (1.7.0). Unchanged:
the carousel, but for the icon it is given (D4); the layout of the grid,
its thumbnails, titles, frames, scroll and live thumbnails; the
configuration.

== Decisions

D1 and D2 the author's, 2026-10-27, after the comparison; D3 the author's,
as recommended; D4 and D5 made in the specification.

D1 (the author's). *72 px.* The icon is drawn in a square of 72 px, three
times the 24 of now, in its proportions.

D2 (the author's). *At the top centre.* The icon is centred across the
tile, 8 px below the top of the thumbnail box — the box `gridThumbnail`
centres the thumbnail in —, not of the thumbnail itself: the icons of a
row stand on one line whatever the proportions of their windows. Over the
thumbnail of a wide window, which the box letterboxes, the icon reaches
above it into the band of the tile's background.

D3 (the author's). *No key.* The size and the place are fixed, as they are
now; the configuration keys of the trial are dropped.

D4. *An icon not upscaled.* `_NET_WM_ICON` is read at the size closest to
128 px, larger rather than smaller — 48 px now —, so that an application
giving several sizes is drawn at 72 by downscaling. The icons by class
already prefer `hicolor/128x128`. The carousel draws the same icon at
48 px times the scale of its card, now from the larger image. The cost: 64
KiB of pixels a window read and kept instead of 9 KiB.

D5. *No larger than the box.* On a tile whose thumbnail box is less than
72 px wide or high the icon is the box's least side.
