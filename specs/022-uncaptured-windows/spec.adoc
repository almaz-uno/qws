== What and why

Some windows keep no thumbnail of the snapshotter (008) however they
change (issue #62): Dion Desktop on ws1, by the author's report, and the
xterm of S5 of specification 020, which printed a line every 20 ms on the
visible workspace of DP-4 and was not captured once in 18 runs of 1.3.2,
while every other window was — the warm-up of `measure.sh` shows each
window in turn (research, "On E1").

The cause, confirmed by a probe off the screen (research, "The probe"): the
snapshotter names the pixmap of a client window with Composite's
`NameWindowPixmap`, and the X server names only the pixmap of a window it
redirects. A compositor redirects the children of the root window — the
frames of i3 — and the X server redirects on its own a window whose visual
differs from its parent's when one of the two is the 32-bit ARGB visual of
Composite. A GTK or an Electron window of depth 32 in its frame is so
redirected and has a pixmap of its own; a window of depth 24 — xterm, Dion,
and the Qt windows of ws1, KeePassXC, Telegram, Yandex Music — is drawn into
the pixmap of its frame and has none. `NameWindowPixmap` answers BadMatch,
which 008 takes for a window not viewable after all: it tries again three
times and then waits for the next change, which fails alike — silently,
for good. On ws1, 4 of the 35 client windows. DAMAGE is not the cause: the
damage of a client window reports drawing into its children, of its own
client and of another, whatever its visual.

The change: a viewable window whose pixmap cannot be named is taken from
the pixmap of its nearest ancestor that can — its frame — by RENDER, the
path of 018, from the rectangle where the window lies in it; without one,
on the CPU, as 008 takes a window nothing else takes.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `759c6d3` (1.3.2). Unchanged:
which windows are captured and when (008, 011); the windows with a pixmap of
their own, captured on the GPU, or by RENDER where the GPU will not bind
them (018), as before; thumbnails of at most 512×512; the configuration —
no key, variable or flag changes.

== Decisions

None taken by the author yet. Each lists its options and the one
recommended — for the author to confirm.

=== D1. How a window without a pixmap of its own is taken

[cols="1,3,3,3"]
|===
| | Option | For | Against

| a
| *From its frame's pixmap, by RENDER.* The pixmap of the nearest
  ancestor redirected is named; the rectangle of the window in it is copied
  in the X server into a pixmap of the window's size and scaled as 018
  scales a window's own.
| Reads only: nothing changes in how the X server and the compositor draw
  the window. The path and its quality are those of 018: 1.6 ms at p50 on
  E1, within a mean of 1.2 per channel of the area average.
| The X server does the work, a copy of the window more than 018. The
  window has no texture on the GPU, so no live thumbnail of 020.

| b
| *Redirected by qws.* The snapshotter redirects the window itself,
  `CompositeRedirectWindow` automatic, and so gives it a pixmap of its own,
  bound on the GPU as any other.
| The GPU path of 008, and live thumbnails of 020, for every window.
| Changes how the X server draws the author's windows for as long as qws
  runs: a pixmap more a window — 14 MB for one of 2556×1357 — a copy into
  the frame at each change, and the window unmapped and mapped again inside
  the X server when redirected, which makes its client draw it anew. A patch
  of 1.3 should not.

| c
| *On the CPU.* `GetImage` of the window, the path of 1.0.0.
| Needs nothing.
| 97–111 ms a window on E1 (018), the cost 008 removed.
|===

*Recommended: a*, with c where no ancestor's pixmap can be named — no
compositor runs — for the author to confirm. Option b is left to 020 as an
open decision: whether live thumbnails of such windows are worth redirecting
them.

=== D2. The frame's pixmap is not bound on the GPU

The pixmap of a frame is the compositor's: picom binds it on ws1, and NVIDIA
lets one GLX pixmap exist on a storage (018). Bound by qws, a compositor
started or restarted after qws could not bind it and would not draw the
window. *Recommended*, for the author to confirm: the frame's pixmap is
taken by RENDER only, never bound on the GPU.

=== D3. Where the window lies in the frame

i3 moves a client window inside its frame — a title bar shown or hidden, a
border — and ICCCM has it send the client synthetic ConfigureNotify events
in root coordinates, which tell nothing of the frame. *Recommended*, for
the author to confirm: the offset is asked of the X server
(`TranslateCoordinates`) at each capture from the frame, one round trip;
the frame's pixmap is named anew when the frame is configured — a new size
is a new pixmap — or mapped, as a window's own is.

== Behavior

. *Naming.* A stale window's pixmap is named as in 008. When the X server
  refuses with BadMatch and the window is viewable, the pixmap of its
  nearest ancestor below the root that can be named is named instead — on
  i3, its frame. A window not viewable is as in 008.
. *Capture from an ancestor.* The rectangle of the window in the ancestor's
  pixmap — its offset from the ancestor's origin, plus the ancestor's
  border — is copied into a pixmap of the window's size and scaled by RENDER
  as in 018, the picture of the ancestor's visual. The GPU is not tried. The
  record "Snapshot" says `render` and the ancestor, `via`.
. *Named anew* when the window or the ancestor is mapped or unmapped, the
  window changes size, or the ancestor is configured.
. *Without an ancestor* whose pixmap can be named, or without RENDER, the
  window is taken on the CPU, as in 008 when a path fails, and logged so.
. *Unchanged:* DAMAGE of the client window, which reports drawing into its
  children; the schedule; the windows with a pixmap of their own.

== Metrics

S1 — cost of a snapshot:: As in 008 and 018: `ms` of the record "Snapshot"
by path, and off the screen `BenchmarkFrameRender`: a client window of
2556×1357 in a frame of 2560×1381, the rectangle copied and scaled.

Scenario E:: On E1, the service replaced by the build or a second instance
beside it: an xterm and Dion on the visible workspace, each changed — a
line printed, a message shown — then the switcher shown: their cards show
what they show, and the log has records "Snapshot" of them, `path` `render`
and `via` their frames.

== Acceptance criteria

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| `TestCaptureFromFrame` in `pkg/snapshot`: a client window of depth 24 in
  an override-redirect frame of its visual off the screen — its picture in a
  child of another client, or of its own — and one of another visual of
  depth 24, and one two levels below the window the compositor redirects:
  each captured, by RENDER from the ancestor, within a mean of 1.2 per
  channel of the area average of what it shows; drawn anew, captured anew,
  the new picture; the ancestor's pixmap not bound on the GPU. It fails
  without the change: no thumbnail. Skipped without an X display, a
  compositing manager, RENDER or offscreen GLX.

| DAMAGE: in the same test, drawing into a child of either client reports
