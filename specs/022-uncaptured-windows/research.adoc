== What the code does

`pkg/snapshot/snapshot.go` of 1.3.2:

* `follow` selects the structure events of a client window of
  `_NET_CLIENT_LIST`, makes a DAMAGE object of it, `ReportLevelNonEmpty`,
  and follows the map state of its parent when that is not the root: the
  frame of i3. `viewable` is the window mapped and its frame mapped.
  `reconcile` follows the windows that appear in the list later. A floating
  window of i3 has a frame of its own, a child of the root, as a tiled one.
* `captureGPU` of a stale window names its pixmap with
  `NameWindowPixmap`; a failure is wrapped as `errNotViewable`, "the X
  server would not name the window's pixmap — it is not viewable after all,
  as while i3 switches workspaces".
* `capture` on `errNotViewable` returns without a snapshot and without a
  record: the window is tried again a settle later, three times, and then
  at its next change, which DAMAGE reports. No path of 018 or of the CPU is
  tried.

== The probe

`capprobe`: windows of its own off every monitor — a frame, an
override-redirect child of the root at −3000,−3000, which picom redirects as
it redirects the frames of i3; in it a client window of 600×400; in that a
child of the same client (as the VT100 widget of xterm) and one of another
connection (as the child of Dion, of another client). From a third
connection, as the snapshotter: `NameWindowPixmap` of the frame and of the
client window, and DAMAGE (`ReportLevelNonEmpty`) of the client window when
each child is drawn into, after a `Subtract`. Each case in a frame of its
own. The visuals of ws1: the default 0x21 of depth 24, 44 TrueColor visuals
of depth 24 and 44 of depth 32, the first of these 0x23, which the frames
of i3 of a window of depth 32 have.

[cols="4,2,2,2",options="header"]
|===
| Client window in its frame | Frame named | Window named | Its damage on drawing into a child

| depth 24, the frame's visual — xterm, Dion, Yandex Music
| yes | BadMatch | yes, either client
| depth 24, another visual than the frame's — KeePassXC, Telegram
| yes | BadMatch | yes, either client
| depth 32, in a frame of depth 24

| depth 32, the frame's visual of depth 32
| yes | BadMatch | yes, either client
| depth 32, another visual than the frame's 0x23 — GTK, Electron on i3

The X server redirects a window on its own — and gives it a pixmap — when
its visual differs from its parent's and one of the two is the ARGB visual
of Composite (`compImplicitRedirect` of the X server); two visuals of depth
24 that differ do not count. A window not redirected is drawn into the
pixmap of its nearest redirected ancestor, its frame, and
`NameWindowPixmap` of it answers BadMatch — as it does for a window not
viewable, so 008 could not tell the two apart. DAMAGE of a client window
reports drawing into its children in every case.

== The frame's pixmap

* It is the compositor's: picom binds the pixmaps of the frames on the GPU,
  and NVIDIA refuses a second GLX pixmap of a storage, 11 on 152.22, while
  the first lives (research of 018, "The refusal"). Bound by qws first —
  picom restarted — it would refuse picom.
* It holds the frame's border and its decoration; the client window lies in
  it at its offset from the frame's origin, `TranslateCoordinates`, plus the
  frame's border. i3 gives the frame no border and the client window none.
* i3 sends a client window synthetic ConfigureNotify events in root
  coordinates (ICCCM, 4.1.5), which the snapshotter receives with the real
  ones, as it selects the structure of the window.
