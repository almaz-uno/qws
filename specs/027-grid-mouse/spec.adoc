== What and why

The mouse hovers and clicks the tiles of the grid through
`getWindowIndexAtPositionGrid` of `pkg/ui/selector.go`, which lays the grid
out again in a copy of its own of `layoutGrid` (`pkg/carousel`). The copy
lays the tiles out over the whole window; the grid is drawn below the band
of the header since specification 005. With the header shown, the tile the
copy finds under the pointer is that of a grid laid out higher: by half the
band where the tiles are of their largest size, and with tiles taller than
those drawn where the height limits them, by more toward the last row. The
hover frame then lands on another tile than the one under the pointer, and
a click activates that other window, or none. Seen in specification 026
(open decisions); 026 exported `carousel.GridColumns`, but the copy keeps
its own column formula too.

The change: the hit test of the grid takes each tile where the grid draws
it, from `carousel.GridTile`, and the copy goes. A fix without new
behaviour: a patch version (constitution, "Releases and versions").

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `53dc14c` (1.4.0). Unchanged:
the hit test of the carousel, the hover and the click as events, the
drawing of both layouts.

== Decisions

D1. *The rectangles of the drawing.* The hit test of the grid asks
`carousel.GridTile` — the rectangle of a tile that the layers of the grid
(specification 007) and its tests take — for each tile, below the band of
the header as the grid is drawn. The bounds stay inclusive and a point in
no tile gives none, as before. Rejected: correcting the copy by the band,
which would leave two layouts of the grid to keep equal.

D2. *The carousel as it is.* Its cards are placed about the centre of the
window, which the header does not move, and its hit test computes them so;
not changed here.

== Behavior

In the grid the tile the mouse hovers and clicks is the tile drawn under the
pointer: with the header on and off, for any number of windows and of
columns. The gaps between the tiles and the band of the header hover none.
