== Approach

*Told of the live thumbnails* (D2). `Snapshotter.PrepareLive`, an atomic
flag the loop reads: from then on the snapshots of the windows taken from
their frames make the pixmaps of their passes. `cmd/qws/root.go` calls it
right after `snapshot.New`, when `appearance.thumbnail.live` is on and the
renderer is `glx`: the snapshotter follows the windows from its start and
captures them a settle later, after the call.

*The pixmap at the snapshot* (D1). `capture`, after a snapshot that
worked, of any cause but an activation's, calls `prepareScaled`
(`pkg/snapshot/live.go`): for a window taken from its frame, when told and
the passes from frames are possible — RENDER there, no pixmap that would
not bind so far — and the window has no pixmap of its thumbnail's size,
`scaledFor` makes and binds it, as the first pass does now, and the record
"Pixmap of the live passes made" gives its time. A failure is the first
pass's to meet again, as now; a binding that fails sets `noScaled` and
says so once, in `scaledFor` as now. The first pass then finds the pixmap
(`scaledFor` returns it) and makes only its chain, its live textures and
the pass. `captureHeld`, the last snapshot of a window hidden, makes none:
a window hidden is not shown.

Nothing else moves: the chain is still made at the first pass of each
activation and dropped at its end; the pixmap is still dropped when the
window is forgotten or made anew at another size; the passes, their
schedule and pictures as they are.

*The benchmarks.* `BenchmarkE1FirstSwitch` (`pkg/ui`) and
`BenchmarkFirstPassBeside` (`pkg/snapshot`) committed before the change,
run against `develop` for T1 (research). With the change,
`BenchmarkFirstPassBeside` runs two variants in the same run — `at-pass`,
not told, as before; `at-snapshot`, told — so that K1 compares them in the
same state of the host.
