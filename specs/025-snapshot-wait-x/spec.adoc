== What and why

A snapshot on the GPU (specification 008) binds the window's pixmap as a
texture — once the pixmap is named, `BindPixmap` of `pkg/glx`, and again at
each capture after, `Rebind`, a release and a bind without a round trip —
and averages it at once. On NVIDIA the X server may not yet have done on the
GPU the drawing it was asked for and has answered: the average then reads
the pixmap as it was before, in whole or in part. That drawing was
subtracted from the window's damage before the capture, so no event brings
the snapshotter back to it: a window that stops changing keeps a picture a
step old until it changes again.

`TestGPUThumbnail` (K4 of 008) shows it: a pixmap drawn anew and bound again
gives the thumbnail of what it held before, "drawn anew … channels differ",
under the load of the author's desktop — 4 of 6 runs on `develop` on
2026-10-02, 8 of 10 on 2026-10-03, 3 of 3 at 17:01 that day (research).
Specification 020, on its branch, found the same in its live passes and made
them wait with `glXWaitX`: `TestSharedLive` failed in 5 of 55 runs without
it and in none with it; of the passes from a frame, 10 of 200 were stale
without it, 0 of 200 with it, 7 of 80 with an `XSync` of the GL display
instead.

The cause is an order GLX does not give: the X stream and the GL stream are
each sequential, but not with each other; the texture bound to a pixmap
holds the rendering completed before the binding, and GLX does no
synchronization between the two (research). The change: the snapshotter
calls `glXWaitX` before each binding of a window's pixmap, the first and
every one after.

The specification is a delta to `develop` at `9774075` (1.3.3). Unchanged:
which windows are captured and when; the paths — the GPU, RENDER, the CPU —
and the choice among them; RENDER and the CPU, which need no wait
(research); the thumbnail and its size; the log; the configuration — no key,
variable or flag changes.

== Decisions

Made on the way, 2026-10-03, for the author to confirm:

* *`glXWaitX`, not a round trip.* A reply — an `XSync` of the GL display, or
  of the connection that drew — orders the X server's requests, not its work
  on the GPU: `TestGPUThumbnail` fails with every strip of its drawing
  answered, and 020 had 7 of 80 passes stale with an `XSync` of the GL
  display. A fence of the X server imported into GL
  (`GL_EXT_x11_sync_object`) would make the GPU wait instead of the thread,
  but needs an object of the X server per snapshotter, requests to trigger
  and reset it at each capture, and an extension NVIDIA wrote; `glXWaitX` is
  in GLX since 1.0 and is what 020 calls.
* *Before the first binding as well.* `GLX_EXT_texture_from_pixmap` defines
  the texture by the rendering completed before `glXBindTexImageEXT`, at the
  first binding as at any other, and the round trips of naming the pixmap
  and of `BindPixmap` order no work on the GPU. No first binding has been
  seen stale — the check "first" of the test has not failed: the wait there
  rests on the extension, not on a measurement.
* *`WaitX` of 020 as it is.* `(*glx.Offscreen).WaitX` and the C function
  under it keep the names and the text of 020's branch — added by `fd2b4a1`,
  the comment as `de4083b` left it — so that 020 merges without a conflict
  there. Its comment names 020's live passes, its test and its numbers,
  which reach `develop` with that merge; the reasons of this change are told
  where `captureGPU` calls it.
* *The test waits as the snapshotter does.* `TestGPUThumbnail` binds through
  `pkg/glx`, not through `captureGPU`, so the change of `captureGPU` does not
  reach it: the test calls `WaitX` before its first binding and before the
  second, as `captureGPU` does. Without it the test would keep failing
  however the snapshotter waits.

== Behavior

. *The wait.* Before it binds the pixmap of a window — `BindPixmap` once the
  pixmap is named, `Rebind` at each capture after — `captureGPU` calls
  `glXWaitX` in the snapshotter's GL context: the drawing the X server has
  executed before it is done before the GL commands after it, and the
  average reads the pixmap as the X server drew it up to the capture.
. *The other paths.* RENDER (018, 022) and the CPU path scale and read the
  pixmap in the X server, ordered after its drawing by the X protocol, and
  get the result in a reply: nothing is added to them.

== Metrics

F — failing runs of `TestGPUThumbnail`:: Two test binaries of
`pkg/snapshot`, `go test -c`: B of `develop` at `9774075`, built before
anything changed, and A of this change. They run in turn, B and A, the one
first alternating from pair to pair, 50 runs each, `-test.run
'^TestGPUThumbnail$'`, on E1 under the load of the author's desktop as it
is, the service running.

P — the snapshotter, a window drawn anew:: A probe outside the repository
(research): a client window of 2556×1357 and depth 32 in a frame of depth
24, off the screen — a window the X server redirects on its own, whose
pixmap the snapshotter binds — drawn anew before each capture, two pictures
in turn, and captured by `capture` of the snapshotter, 200 times. A
thumbnail more than a mean of 1.2 per channel from the area average of the
picture drawn is stale. Built with B's code and with A's, run in turn.

S1 — cost of a snapshot:: In the same probe, the wall time of `capture` at
p50 and p95, 200 captures each: drawn anew just before, and at rest —
captured again with nothing drawn since.

== Acceptance criteria

[cols="1,6"]
|===
| # | Criterion and method

| K1
| The test steady: in F, A fails none of its 50 runs, while B fails at
  least 10 of its 50; with fewer, the load did not reproduce the issue, and
  F is run again.

| K2
| The snapshotter: in P, none of A's 200 captures is stale; B's stale
  captures are counted.

| K3
| Cost: S1 at rest, A's p50 no more than 0.5 ms above B's. Drawn anew, S1 is
  recorded without a bound: there A waits for the drawing its thumbnail
  shows.

| K4
| Unchanged: `TestCaptureFromFrame`, `TestRenderThumbnail`, `TestFallback`,
  `TestNewAndClose`, `TestSchedule` pass.

| K5
| `go vet ./...`, `go test ./...`, `go test -race ./pkg/snapshot`,
  `gofmt -l .` empty and `make check-specs` pass; no `Init` of an extension
  of xgb and no `xgb.NewConn` outside `pkg/x11/extensions.go` and the tests
  (021).
|===
